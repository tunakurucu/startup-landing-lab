# Measured homepages · 2026-10-19

`measurements.jsonl` holds one record per homepage, produced by [`scripts/measure.mjs`](../scripts/measure.mjs): headless Chromium, one visit per viewport (1440 × 900 and 390 × 844), robots.txt respected, numbers only. No markup, assets or screenshots are stored. 225 homepages were attempted: 205 candidates from YC, a16z and Pear plus the 20 earlier audited sites. 211 returned complete measurements; 14 refused automated visits (HTTP 403/405/502, a robots.txt disallow, or a timeout), and those are recorded with the error. The table and lines below are generated by [`scripts/summarize_measurements.py`](../scripts/summarize_measurements.py) (nearest-rank percentiles); validation fails if they drift from the data.

Known limits found while auditing against these numbers: `sticky_nav` misses a nav pinned through a wrapper element (several reference sites were recorded false while their bar stayed pinned); `nav_height_px` is wrong where the header element wraps the hero; a page that scrolls inside an inner container (for example mmm.page) reports a one-screen height and zero sections; `dark_background` comes from the first-screen capture and can disagree with what a person sees on pages whose hero loads late; `animations_on_load` counts animations the browser reports, including ones with no visible effect. Audited records note each disagreement in their limitations.

## How to read this

These are computed styles, not verified font identities: the family is the first name in the computed stack, which can be a site's alias. Animation counts are animations running at that moment, not an inventory of motion. Section counts depend on markup. The sample is YC 2026, a16z (fund and speedrun) and Pear companies plus earlier audited sites, so it describes what these startups ship, not what converts.

The archetype recipes now quote these numbers as **observed** next to their **recommended** values. The recommended headline cap (about 6.5rem, 104 px) sits just above the 90th percentile; the recommended tracking floor (−0.04em) sits between the 10th and 25th percentiles.
