# Research method and evidence

This edition contains **32 candidates, 27 verified YC company records and eight written audit records**. Four audits include full-page desktop inspection; four are partial desktop field notes. No audit has complete desktop/tablet/mobile and reduced-motion coverage. Do not describe this as 12–15 completed deep audits. Four more San Francisco candidates have [source recon](source-recon-2026-10-19.md), not rendered audits.

Five candidates are Bay Area comparisons outside the verified-YC set. Their geography evidence is recorded individually. Their YC status is **not verified**, which does not establish non-membership. Two of these comparisons have audit records. The gallery exposes this distinction rather than calling unknown status “non-YC.”

## Canonical data

- [candidates.json](candidates.json): company identity, source website, industry, official YC evidence where available, geography evidence, and audit join.
- [sites.json](sites.json): canonical observations, interpretations, limitations and sources for inspected websites.
- [candidate schema](schema/candidates.schema.json) and [audit schema](schema/sites.schema.json): machine-readable contracts.
- [audits](audits): readable views generated by `python3 scripts/generate.py` from the repository root.
- [pattern index](patterns/README.md): synthesis used by the standalone skill.
- [2026 YC audit queue](2026-yc-audit-queue.md): verified candidates for the next new visual inspections.

The gallery imports sites.json directly. The skill's bundled casebook and readable audit documents are generated snapshots. Edit canonical data, regenerate, and run `npm run validate`. Never edit generated audit prose alone.

## Selection and inspection

Official public YC company pages supplied verified company identity, batch, website and location fields. Displayed public page metadata was read directly; no private endpoint was accessed. Identity was checked rather than inferred from URL slugs. `/companies/ramp` returned Paribus and `/companies/tally` returned Kips Health, so those were rejected for the intended companies. Cal.com could not be verified from the attempted official listing and was excluded.

Candidates cover technical tools, analytics, consumer marketplaces, hardware, operations, fintech and healthcare. Recent verified examples include HumanLayer F24, Onlook W25, Diode S24 and LightSprint P26. Their inclusion is not a statement about investment quality. Companies may have outgrown startup status; public and acquired companies remain labelled by official metadata where available.

Rendered browser inspection on October 18, 2026 used a 1363 × 936 desktop viewport. We inspected headings and page sequence, hero composition, selected lower sections, and specific interaction states. Firecrawl, Raycast, Stripe and Resend received full-page composition captures. Other records retain partial coverage. These captures were temporary research evidence and are not redistributed. On October 19, first-party source pages were read for Figma, Cursor, Notion and Webflow. New rendered browser calls timed out, so their source recon remains outside the audit data and gallery.

## Evidence policy

Observable facts live under `observations`; our interpretation lives under `interpretation`. Every record carries viewports, coverage, date, limitations and sources. Motion is claimed only for an action actually observed. Duration, easing and implementation library are unverified. Full-page captures may contain unrevealed scroll content; blank regions do not prove broken content.

Onlook displayed a hero scene error in this browser. Its fallback and FAQ state were observed; the scene itself was not evaluated. Some initially exposed Raycast controls could not be found after hydration, so their behavior is unverified. No forms, emails, accounts, payments or remote scrapes were submitted.

The gallery uses original abstract composition studies, not copied screenshots, logos or proprietary assets. Research is selective, time-bound and not a ranking. No claim about conversions, universal accessibility or superior design performance follows from these observations.

## Extend the collection

Use [the audit template](audit-template.md) and [contribution guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md). Verify the company, inspect the rendered page, separate observation from inference, retain limitations, and record the inspection date. A site can remain a useful partial record without pretending to be complete.
