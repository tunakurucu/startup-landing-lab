# Research method and evidence

This edition contains **679 candidates and 130 audit records**. 603 candidates come from YC (W25 to F26), a16z (2026 fund rounds and speedrun SR001–SR007), Pear, Sequoia Arc, Neo and Entrepreneur First, and each was verified on the program's own page; candidate leads came from those programs' public directories and posts. A few Pear entries show no year on Pear's page and are labelled that way. A further 42 candidates form a **reference tier**: product companies chosen for the design of their sites, each with its selection signal recorded, measured separately and never mixed into the cohort percentiles. **110 of the records were audited on October 19–20, 2026** at 1440 and 390 px: rendered, scrolled, one safe control operated, reloaded with reduced motion, and measured with [`scripts/measure.mjs`](../scripts/measure.mjs). The other 20 records are earlier desktop inspections; sixteen are full-page and four are partial field notes. 328 cohort homepages carry computed-style measurements ([summary](measurements.md)); a motion sweep with [`scripts/measure-motion.mjs`](../scripts/measure-motion.mjs) is recorded in `motion.jsonl`.

Five candidates are Bay Area comparisons outside the verified-YC set. Their geography evidence is recorded individually. Their YC status is **not verified**, which does not establish non-membership. Two of these comparisons have audit records. The gallery exposes this distinction rather than calling unknown status “non-YC.”

## Findings from the October 19 audits

- **Reduced motion is widely ignored.** With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` requested, 11 of the 32 audited sites kept visible motion running (carousels, demo timers, scroll-driven sequences, playing video), and one hid its entire hero. Three more showed minor residual change. The skill's MO-01 and MO-02 checks exist for this.
- **Headlines are smaller and lighter than generated pages suggest.** Across 211 measured homepages the median desktop h1 is 64 px at weight 500 with −0.025em tracking. Generated pages tend toward 80–100 px at 700–800.
- **The strongest pages put the product's own output in the first screen**: a search box with real listings (Snag), a prefilled request (Context.dev), a replayed failure (Canary), a resolved exception (End Close), a live call (Agentphone). This is the most repeated principle in the new records.
- **Some pages are deliberately almost nothing.** Traverse is an unstyled text memo, Known a short manifesto. These work when the sale happens through a few direct relationships, and the records say when they would not.
- **Breakage in the wild.** One homepage crashed into an error panel on scroll, one hid sections below a certain point, and one primary "Preorder" action led to a sold-out page. Each is recorded as observed, with its limits.

## Selection and inspection

Official public YC company pages supplied verified company identity, batch, website and location fields. Displayed public page metadata was read directly; no private endpoint was accessed. Identity was checked rather than inferred from URL slugs. `/companies/ramp` returned Paribus and `/companies/tally` returned Kips Health, so those were rejected for the intended companies. Cal.com could not be verified from the attempted official listing and was excluded.

Candidates cover technical tools, analytics, consumer marketplaces, hardware, operations, fintech and healthcare. Recent verified examples include HumanLayer F24, Onlook W25, Diode S24 and LightSprint P26. Their inclusion is not a statement about investment quality. Companies may have outgrown startup status; public and acquired companies remain labelled by official metadata where available.

Rendered browser inspection on October 18, 2026 used a 1363 × 936 desktop viewport. Firecrawl, Raycast, Stripe and Resend received full-page composition captures. The October 19 inspections of LightSprint, Palette, Sherpa, Nori and Cyclon used the same desktop viewport, full-page scans, selected viewport screenshots and operated controls. Full-page captures of scroll-triggered or media-heavy sections sometimes showed empty frames; we traversed selected sections separately and recorded playback failures. The seven later 2025-batch inspections used the same viewport, rendered hero and selected sections, accessibility output, and one visible interaction each. Some full-page captures left scroll-triggered areas blank; the records state which details were observed through rendered sections and which remain unverified. The older partial records retain partial coverage. These captures were temporary research evidence and are not redistributed. First-party source pages for Figma, Cursor, Notion and Webflow were read before the 2026 YC scope change; their recon remains outside the audit data and gallery.

## Evidence policy

Observable facts live under `observations`; our interpretation lives under `interpretation`. Every record carries viewports, coverage, date, limitations and sources. The documented compliments are dated selection signals. They do not prove the current site is identical to a praised earlier version, nor that it converts well. Motion is claimed only for an action actually observed. Duration, easing and implementation library are unverified. Full-page captures may contain unrevealed scroll content; blank regions do not prove broken content.

Onlook displayed a hero scene error in this browser. Its fallback and FAQ state were observed; the scene itself was not evaluated. Some initially exposed Raycast controls could not be found after hydration, so their behavior is unverified. No forms, emails, accounts, payments or remote scrapes were submitted.

The gallery uses original abstract composition studies, not copied screenshots, logos or proprietary assets. Research is selective, time-bound and not a ranking. No claim about conversions, universal accessibility or superior design performance follows from these observations.

## Extend the collection

Use [the audit template](audit-template.md) and [contribution guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md). Verify the company, inspect the rendered page, separate observation from inference, retain limitations, and record the inspection date. A site can remain a useful partial record without pretending to be complete.
