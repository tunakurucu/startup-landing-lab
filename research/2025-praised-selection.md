# Seven 2025 YC sites with a documented design signal

Selected and inspected 2026-10-19 at 1363 × 936. “2025” means the **YC batch year**, not necessarily the founding year. All seven have an official YC company page. A design compliment is a lead worth inspecting, not evidence of conversion performance or an endorsement of the current version.

Five appeared in [Rishabh Sambare’s public list of favorite recent YC landing pages](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/rishabhsamb_i-found-my-favorite-landing-pages-from-recent-activity-7378140765811568640-oUaI). Interfere appeared in a [curated design gallery](https://designmunk.com/interfere/). Toothy had a [design-vendor case study](https://www.awesomic.com/case-study/toothy-redesign), which is promotional and not independent praise. The audits link both those signals and the current site.

| Current site | YC evidence | Selection signal | What the live audit actually found |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Airweave](audits/airweave.md) | [P25](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/airweave) | Roundup: clean dark visuals | Dark API-led hero; cURL tab changed the code example. |

The historical praise describes a different version. |
| [AnswerThis](audits/answerthis.md) | [F25](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/answerthis) | Roundup: favorite then-current F25 page | A long citation and research-workflow story; Features menu opened. The hero video buffered in the first observation. |
| [Pingo](audits/pingo.md) | [S25](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/pingo) | Roundup: colorful, clear CTA | Present page is quieter, with sample conversations and store actions; FAQ expanded. |
| [Cactus](audits/cactus.md) | [P25](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/oncactus) | Roundup: consistent cactus theme | Current trade-focused page retains its character, adds job-site footage, an audit form and a revenue calculator. Hero carousel changed state. |
| [Interfere](audits/interfere.md) | [S25](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/interfere) | DesignMunk featured its homepage | A recurring broken-password incident anchors the current product narrative; Product menu opened. |
| [Toothy AI](audits/toothy.md) | [W25](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/toothy-ai) | Vendor documented a previous redesign | Current page uses verification and billing panels plus pricing tabs. Both modes changed on click. Vendor claims are not independent validation. |

The publicly praised [Proliferate](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/proliferate) now shows only a “more coming soon” screen. [Propolis](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/propolis) failed to load in the inspecting browser after a retry. [Weave](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/weave-3) returned an intermittent page-load error and rendered blank in that browser despite accessible text. [Sunset](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/sunset) and [Origami](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/origami-agents) belong to 2024 YC batches. They were not counted to reach twenty.

These are desktop inspections, with selected visible interactions and explicit limits in each audit. The gallery uses original abstract marks rather than redistributing startup screenshots. Claims about customers, compliance, revenue or product effectiveness belong to the companies and were not independently verified.
