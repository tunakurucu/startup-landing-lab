## Observations

### Hero

Left column: a 'New · Granola for Apple Watch' pill, a four-line serif h1, a two-line sans subhead, a dark olive 'Download for free ↓' pill and a platform line ('Available for macOS, Windows, iOS, Android'). Right: a 'Q3 GTM sync' note window with traffic-light dots over collage-style art panels, overlapped by a phone-shaped call window and a 'Transcribing' pill.

### Typography

Measured h1 86.4px, weight 400, line-height 0.9, tracking −0.02em, family quadrant (a serif in the captures); body 16px melange (a sans) at 1.5, about 50ch. The serif recurs in section heads, mock note titles and testimonial quotes; sans carries UI text and body. 'For the doers' is set near full content width. Phone h1 48px; body stays 16px.

### Visual System

White ground with near-black text. Brand colour is a dark olive button plus lime-yellow panels and charcoal bands. Imagery mixes macOS-style app windows, call video tiles, halftone and abstract collage art, and landscape photos behind feature mocks. Full-width bands separate chapters (charcoal logos, lime 'Perfect meeting memory', charcoal testimonials, warm grey MCP). The footer ends in a giant Granola wordmark.

### Page Sequence

Hero → 'Effortless notes, enhanced instantly.' with three proof rows → oversized 'For the doers' → charcoal 'Trusted by teams we admire' logo grid → 'Granola helps you before, during and after your meetings.' three-step stepper with panels → lime 'Perfect meeting memory' chat box → charcoal testimonials → 'Works everywhere you do, how you do' five feature rows → 'Use your meeting notes anywhere' MCP connector → 'Unlimited meeting notes for free' card CTA → footer with wordmark.

### Copy

H1: 'The AI notepad for back-to-back meetings'. Subhead: 'Notes, actions and memory. Without a meeting bot.' Primary button 'Download for free' (nav adds 'Talk to sales'). The category is in the h1 ('AI notepad'); the audience is people in stacked meetings ('For the doers', 'Helping busy people through busy days'), and the no-bot difference recurs ('Humans in the room, not bots').

### Interaction

Clicked 'After the meeting' in the stepper: the page scrolled about 1,770px to the 'Post-meeting admin, done' panel, the label turned dark with an underline, and the panel showed 'List actions', 'Write follow-up email' and 'Draft project plan'. On load the hero note typed text, showed an 'Enhancing notes' state, and the call tiles changed faces. With reduced motion set, the note still typed and showed 'Enhancing notes' between captures.

### Accessibility

Inactive stepper labels and the platform line are light grey on white. In several live captures the nav download pill rendered as an empty grey bar with no text; at 390, a 112×40px link with no text was found and four unlabelled controls were counted. The 'New' pill link is 32px tall on phone. No horizontal overflow at either width.

### Mobile

H1 drops to 48px, left-aligned over four lines; the full 'Download for free' button stays in the first screen and the note mock moves below it, with the art panels cropped to its edges. The nav collapses to a 'Download' pill and a menu icon. Feature rows and testimonials stack to one column; measured sections drop to 10 and page height to 12,192px.

## Interpretation

- Carry one named sample artifact (a specific meeting note) through every workflow stage so the visitor follows one story instead of a feature list.
- Put the main objection-killer in the subhead ('Without a meeting bot') and repeat it in the feature rows.
- Let one characterful display serif and one saturated accent hold the brand, and keep product UI in a plain sans so the two never compete.

Suits single-purpose productivity tools with a clear before/during/after and a desktop UI worth showing. Wrong for multi-product platforms, or buyers who need architecture and compliance detail before anything else.

Risk: Much of the meaning sits in images and video; when they had not loaded, sections showed empty frames and an unlabelled nav button. The hero demo ignores reduced motion. The 86px serif h1 takes four lines and pushes the platform line toward the fold.

## Limitations

- On the live visit several images, videos, partner logos and the nav download label loaded late or not at all; imagery is described from the measurement capture.
- Did not dismiss the cookie dialog that appeared over the hero.
- No control was operated at 390 wide.
- Single browser, single inspection date; homepage content can change.
