## Observations

### Hero

On a near-black ground with a deep blue glow, a left-aligned two-line headline reads 'The living library of interfaces'; 'living' is a blue italic serif. A one-line count of '12,000+ crafted React components…' follows. Next comes a '2,000+ Marketing blocks:' label with seven category pills, then a full-bleed row of preview cards (glass blob, AI-tools text hero, line-art figure) that bleeds off both edges.

### Typography

The h1 measures 64px General Sans, weight 500, −0.022em tracking, 1.1 line height. The largest measured text is 69.1px Averia Serif Libre, which matches the italic serif accent words ('living', 'builders', 'anywhere', 'engineers', 'humans', 'answered'). Body text is 17px General Sans at 1.5 line height and a 52ch measure. ui-monospace is used in code panels. Large later headings set a white lead phrase against a grey continuation.

### Visual System

The ground is near-black, with a blue radial glow in the hero and a saturated blue for the Sign up and Browse components buttons. Imagery is the product itself: component preview cards, a code editor, a bookmark list and an author grid with avatars and component counts. The 'Copy the prompt' section breaks palette with three coloured cards: terracotta for Claude Code, blue-violet for Codex, cream-pink for Lovable.

### Page Sequence

Hero with marketing-block pills and preview row → UI-component pills and second preview row → 'Used by 4,226,417 builders' with enterprise and startup logos → 'Copy the prompt. Paste it anywhere' (3 tool cards) → two panels (real code; save for later) → 'Built by real design engineers' author grid fading out → 'Built by humans / Ready for agents' CTA pair → FAQ (7 items) → footer.

### Interaction

I clicked the 'Hero sections' pill. The active state moved to it, and the preview row swapped to different cards: an AI-cloud deploy hero, a 'Take a screenshot' hero and the glass blob. On load the cards first showed as empty placeholders, then filled in. On two desktop visits the page turned into a 'Something went wrong' panel once scrolling began. A reduced-motion load rendered normally; first-screen frames 2s apart were identical, though 18 animations were still reported running.

### Accessibility

Secondary text is grey on dark blue or near-black: pill labels, the grey half of large headings, and author rows that fade toward near-invisible. Category pills are small 13px text. The scroll-triggered error replaced all content, including navigation, with only 'Try again' and 'Go home'. On the stored 390 capture the pill rows are clipped mid-word at the right edge as in-element scrollers; no page-level horizontal overflow was measured.

### Mobile

Nav links disappear, leaving the logo, Log in and Sign up. The h1 drops to 40px over three lines. Both pill rows become clipped horizontal strips, and each preview row shows about one and a half cards. Logos wrap into rows, the three prompt cards and the two panels stack, and the author grid becomes two columns ending with '…and 700+ design engineers'. The FAQ heading sits above the list. The live mobile load showed the error panel; the layout here is from the stored capture.

## Interpretation

- If the inventory is the product, put a filter control and real items in the first screen so the first click is already browsing.
- Show where the product lands (terminal, agent, builder) with one card per destination tool, so 'works with your stack' becomes concrete.
- Give an aggregator a human face by listing contributors with counts; it signals curation and supply depth at once.
- Use one contrasting italic serif for a single word per heading as a recurring brand signature, and keep everything else in one sans.

Marketplaces, template libraries, asset stores and registries whose catalogue is self-explanatory when shown. It would be wrong for a single-workflow B2B tool, where a shelf of thumbnails would hide the one job it does.

Risk: The page depends on heavy client rendering with video and canvas. In this inspection it failed into a full-page error on scroll and on mobile load, which removes everything including navigation. Rows of moving previews compete with each other. The grey half-headlines and faded author grid trade legibility for atmosphere.

## Limitations

- The client-side error appeared on every desktop visit after scrolling and on the mobile load; desktop sections below the second preview row were described from the stored full-page capture, not the live page.
- Could not verify whether the error is specific to headless Chromium or the proxy environment.
- Did not open the FAQ items or any component pages.
- Single browser, single inspection date; homepage content can change.
