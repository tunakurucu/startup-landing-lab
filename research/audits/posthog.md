Inspected: 2026-10-18 · partial-desktop · expressive-world

A product suite presented as a place to explore, rather than a stack of interchangeable cards.

## Company evidence

YC: verified-yc; batch: W20.

- desktop: 1363 × 936: rendered inspection
- tablet: not inspected
- mobile: not inspected

## Observations

### Hero

A desktop-like shell surrounds a central window. Navigation icons sit on both sides; the introduction and signup panel share a row.

### Typography

Bold compact sans-serif headings; body text stays conversational. Colored inline highlights reinforce key terms.

### Visual System

Green illustrated environment, translucent pale window, orange beveled actions and vividly colored story tabs.

### Page Sequence

Introduction → illustrative product tabs → tool inventory → customers → data warehouse → pricing → company rationale → reading → deliberately playful closing CTA.

### Interaction

Clicked Ship with PostHog. The selected tab and product story changed, with sample inbox reports replacing the earlier assistant story. A pause control was present but not tested.

### Accessibility

Semantic structure inspected through browser accessibility output. No contrast, keyboard, screen-reader or reduced-motion audit was completed.

## Interpretation

- Give a broad suite a navigable mental model.
- Let expressive brand elements organize information instead of merely decorating it.
- Identify illustrative data as illustrative.

Choose this approach only if it explains the audience’s actual evaluation task.

Risk: Desktop chrome competes for attention; nested scrolling needs separate mobile and keyboard testing.

## Limitations

- Single browser, single inspection date; homepage content can change.
- Exact font names, animation timing and implementation libraries were not verified.
- Tablet, mobile and reduced-motion behavior remain unverified.
- Only selected regions were visually inspected; section sequence also uses rendered DOM. This is not a complete deep audit.
