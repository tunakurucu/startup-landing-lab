# FLORA

Inspected: 2026-10-20 · desktop-mobile-audit · canvas-first

https://flora.ai/

Generated imagery scattered on a dotted canvas grid is the hero, and the page proves the product with a draggable node graph and one workflow graph per creative discipline.

## Company evidence

YC: not-verified; batch: not verified. Reference tier: recognised for design quality and audited as a benchmark, not as part of the cohort sample.

- desktop: 1440 × 900: rendered, scrolled, one control operated
- tablet: not inspected
- mobile: 390 × 844: rendered and measured

## Observations

### Hero

Black ground with a faint dot grid. A scattered collage of generated fashion and product images (a bag, lips with a 'made in FLORA' mark, red shoes, portraits) floats at the edges around a centred block: h1, one-line subhead, green 'Get started for free' and outlined 'Copy setup prompt' buttons, and 'Works with' four AI-app icons. A greyscale customer logo strip runs along the bottom; a cookie panel overlays the lower middle.

### Typography

Measured h1 52px, weight 400, tracking -0.04em, line-height 1.2, family Marketing Redaction 10 (a serif), with 'creative' in italic; at 390 that word renders with a visibly pixelated texture. Headings and body use measured family Geist (a sans): h2s about 30px, weight 500; step titles such as '01 Ideate' 60px with grey numerals. Body 20px/1.4, 16px/1.3 at 390. A third family, Marketing Redaction 35, is in the families list.

### Visual System

Near-black throughout, with the dotted canvas grid as a recurring motif. Colour comes from generated imagery: fashion photos, product renders, cinematic stills. Product evidence is shown as node graphs: image nodes labelled with model names (Nano, Nano Pro, Veo 3.1, Aleph) joined by thin curves. The use-case chapter puts a muted full-bleed photo behind its graph; model providers appear as photographic tiles with logos. The only accent is green on primary buttons and links.

### Page Sequence

Hero collage → logo marquee → 'Never let a good idea go unexplored.' 01 Ideate / 02 Iterate / 03 Scale cards → 'Every model. One canvas.' draggable node demo → 'Generative workflows that scale.' scroll-switched discipline list with graph → Techniques → Featured Techniques (six creator cards) → 'One subscription to rule them all.' model-provider grid → case studies → 'Read about us.' press → 'A new medium needs a new canvas.' + footer.

### Copy

H1: 'Your creative workspace'. Subhead: 'Purpose-built AI tools for every step of your process.' Primary button 'Get started for free'; secondary 'Copy setup prompt'. The audience is creative teams ('Teams from Pentagram to Lionsgate'; 'professionals across film, fashion, branding, and design'), and the category is framed as a canvas over many models: 'One plan. 50+ models.' Use cases are named by discipline: Visual Effects, Fashion, Advertising, Photography, Concepting, Branding, Motion, Architecture.

### Interaction

Dragged the 'Star Shoe' node in the canvas demo (labelled 'Move Star Shoe node'): it moved 210px right and 180px up, stayed slightly enlarged, and its connector curve re-routed to the Sneaker Showcase node. On load the logo strip scrolled continuously. Scrolling the use-case list highlighted one discipline at a time and swapped the backdrop photo and graph. With reduced motion no animations ran and top and mid-page frames were identical.

### Accessibility

No horizontal overflow; a 'Skip to content' link is present, and the desktop probe found no unnamed buttons or links and no sampled text under 4.5:1. Inactive discipline names are dimmed grey over the photo. At 390, 70 of 94 visible targets were under 44px tall (technique title links 18px, the footer 'Cookie settings' button 16px); the menu button is 40×40 and labelled 'Menu'. The cookie panel covers the h1 at 390.

### Mobile

The h1 stays 52px and breaks to three lines, with collage images crowding the edges; the nav reduces to logo and menu. Sections are reordered and retitled: the node demo follows the hero under 'Take your work further.' with nodes stacked vertically; the discipline list becomes stacked cards, each with title, graph and caption; the model grid becomes a sideways card row; the 01/02/03 cards move lower under 'Create at the speed of thought.' Page height 16,306px.

## Interpretation

- Make the hero a sample of output, a collage of generated work on the product's own grid, so quality is shown before any claim.
- Embed a small, real piece of the product's interaction (draggable nodes with live connectors) in the marketing page.
- Map use cases to the buyer's disciplines and give each one concrete workflow graph, switched by scroll on desktop and stacked on mobile.
- Recompose sections for mobile, including reordering and retitling them, rather than only shrinking the desktop layout.

Suits creative and generative tools whose output is visual and whose buyers judge by taste; wrong for data or back-office products where output is not visually persuasive.

Risk: A long dark page (10,854px) of image-heavy grids; node labels inside the graphs are too small to read at either width; dimmed list items over photos lower legibility; the pixel-textured italic in the 390 h1 trades crispness for style; a wall of third-party model logos dates quickly as providers change.

## Limitations

- Whether media inside the node demo (the Sneaker Showcase output changed between captures) responds to the drag or plays on its own was not determined.
- The desktop full-page capture stops at 9,000px of a 10,854px page; the end was seen in live captures.
- Single browser, single inspection date; homepage content can change.
