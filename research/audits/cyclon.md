# Cyclon

Inspected: 2026-10-19 · desktop-audit · functional-entrance

https://cyclon.ai/

A short waitlist page uses a single phone image and three plain-language capabilities to introduce a new device.

## Company evidence

YC: verified-yc; batch: F26.

- desktop: 1363 × 936: rendered hero, full-page scan, selected sections and interaction
- tablet: not inspected
- mobile: 390 × 844: computed styles measured 2026-10-19; not visually inspected

## Observations

### Hero

Centered, bold two-line statement on white with brief explanatory copy, an email waitlist form and YC backing directly beneath. A tall front-view phone image extends below the fold; navigation is only the logo and About us.

### Typography

Black heavy sans display and smaller gray sans body; the few section labels are much quieter than the hero. Exact family unverified.

### Visual System

Monochrome white/black with an orange YC accent and a metallic device render. Wide whitespace keeps the page short; no card system or elaborate gradient is used.

### Page Sequence

Hero statement and waitlist → phone render → three short capability columns (build apps, connect services, delegate across apps) → concluding belief statement → sparse About/Privacy/Terms/Contact footer.

### Interaction

Opened About us from the header; navigation reached /about and displayed founders and background. The waitlist form was visible but no email was entered or submitted. No product demonstration was available on the homepage.

### Accessibility

The waitlist input has an accessible Email address label, and there is a Skip to content link. Submission behavior, error messaging, keyboard traversal and screen-reader behavior were not tested.

## Interpretation

- Use a single clear action when the product is still collecting interest.
- Give a new hardware concept a recognizable physical anchor.
- Keep early claims proportionate to what a page can show.

Useful for a prelaunch device where the immediate user decision is whether to join a waitlist.

Risk: The page leaves product reality, availability and proof largely unanswered. A phone render and capability statements are not evidence of a functioning device.

## Limitations

- One cloud Chromium session at a 1363 × 936 desktop viewport; the site can change after this date.
- Tablet, mobile, keyboard-only, screen-reader, color contrast and reduced-motion behavior were not tested.
- Exact font families, animation timing, implementation details and conversion effects were not independently established.
- No email was submitted; form validation and confirmation remain unverified. The page has no product interaction to operate beyond navigation.
