## Observations

### Hero

A broad left-aligned typographic statement overlays a colorful diagonal field; signup actions and a logo strip form a clear lower boundary.

### Typography

Large regular sans-serif statement with differences in text tone inside the same paragraph.

### Visual System

White base, precise thin rules, purple actions and a large multicolor diagonal visual.

### Page Sequence

Hero → business-model solutions → global scale → customer segments/cases → developer infrastructure → news → CTA/footer.

### Interaction

Opened Products. The expanded navigation grouped payment capabilities and revealed individual product destinations. Solution demonstrations were not tested.

### Accessibility

Semantic structure inspected through browser accessibility output. No contrast, keyboard, screen-reader or reduced-motion audit was completed.

## Interpretation

- Structure a broad platform by customer decisions.
- Use a large visual field to create direction without enclosing every sentence in a card.
- Separate business narrative from developer integration detail.

Choose this approach only if it explains the audience’s actual evaluation task.

Risk: This mature platform’s breadth is inappropriate for a narrow early-stage product. Homepage claims were observed, not independently verified.

## Limitations

- Single browser, single inspection date; homepage content can change.
- Exact font names, animation timing and implementation libraries were not verified.
- Tablet, mobile and reduced-motion behavior remain unverified.
- Full-page composition and the expanded navigation were inspected on desktop. Some scroll-triggered visuals remained blank in the full-page capture; their behavior is unverified.
