## Observations

### Hero

A white translucent left panel promises an AI revenue engine built for trades over a rotating job-site video backdrop. The narrow webinar strip and top-bar demo action remain visible.

### Typography

Large plain sans-serif claims, uppercase labels and a few heavier emphasized words. The practical home-service wording matters more than type novelty; exact family unverified.

### Visual System

Dark evergreen text, orange linework, real trade footage and a hand-drawn cactus figure. Customer logos and team photo make the page feel located in its specific market.

### Page Sequence

Video hero and customer logos → team/news → three revenue leaks and an audit form → sample call with cactus character → inbound/outbound workflow → revenue-impact sliders → customer quotes → final demo/phone choice and footer.

### Interaction

Selected a hero carousel indicator and observed its selected slide change; automatic rotation also advanced the slide during inspection. No call, form submission or calculator assumption validation was made.

### Accessibility

Hero slider indicators appeared as named checkboxes in the browser accessibility output. Automatic rotation, keyboard operation of the slider, video controls and reduced motion were not tested.

## Interpretation

- Use imagery and language from the buyer’s actual work environment.
- Turn a broad automation promise into missed-call, follow-up and CRM work.
- Show calculator assumptions beside any output and avoid presenting estimates as measured outcomes.

Useful for a vertical service with an explicit revenue-loss diagnosis and a human-assisted sale.

Risk: The calculator output and several ROI statistics are company-provided claims; they were not validated. Automatic video and carousel motion can distract and needs separate accessibility review. The older praise was for a prior page snapshot.

## Limitations

- One cloud Chromium session at a 1363 × 936 desktop viewport on 2026-10-19; the homepage can change.
- Tablet, mobile, keyboard-only, screen-reader, color contrast and reduced-motion behavior were not tested.
- Exact font names, animation timing, implementation libraries and conversion effects were not established.
- A third-party design mention is a selection lead, not an independent conversion or product-quality endorsement.
- The roundup praised its cactus theme. The currently rendered page still uses the character, but its hero has shifted toward job-site footage.
