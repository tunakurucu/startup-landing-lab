## Observations

### Hero

Centered statement and signup/demo actions sit over a landscape with a desk in the foreground. A financial-service disclaimer is visible at the bottom.

### Typography

Clean sans-serif rather than an assumed editorial serif. Medium-weight heading contrasts with lighter supporting copy.

### Visual System

Landscape imagery, muted sky tones, dark navy product regions and electric blue primary actions.

### Page Sequence

Immersive hero → product categories → customer proof → onboarding and capability explanations → company narrative/news → business/personal actions → legal footnotes.

### Interaction

Opened Products. A large multicolumn menu exposed banking, cards, payments and intelligence categories. No account application or demo transaction was submitted.

### Accessibility

Semantic structure inspected through browser accessibility output. No contrast, keyboard, screen-reader or reduced-motion audit was completed.

## Interpretation

- Pair an aspirational opening with concrete product explanation.
- Keep material product qualifications close to the promise.
- Give a broad offer a navigable category structure.

Choose this approach only if it explains the audience’s actual evaluation task.

Risk: Foreground text competes with the landscape. Navigation overlay obscured some lower-page review; full-page and responsive inspection still needed.

## Limitations

- Single browser, single inspection date; homepage content can change.
- Exact font names, animation timing and implementation libraries were not verified.
- Tablet, mobile and reduced-motion behavior remain unverified.
- Only selected regions were visually inspected; section sequence also uses rendered DOM. This is not a complete deep audit.
