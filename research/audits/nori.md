## Observations

### Hero

The robot fills most of a sage-green opening field. Cream badges mark fall 2026 shipping and YC backing; chunky dark NORI A3 lettering and a $1,688 claim anchor the left. Contact and Order remain in a small top bar.

### Typography

Rounded, heavy display letterforms contrast with plain body type and monospaced uppercase task/spec labels. Exact font families unverified.

### Visual System

Olive and off-white fields shift to black documentary footage blocks, then return to cream specification cards. Hardware photography/rendering carries identity; pill badges and dark outlined actions repeat.

### Page Sequence

Product hero → everyday-task selector → task footage collage → skills marketplace → hardware imagery → six specification cards → San Francisco assembly, price, shipping and order CTA → minimal footer. Full-page capture showed large blank regions around scroll media; selected viewport states and accessible structure were checked separately.

### Interaction

Selected Fetching from the fridge in the everyday-task tabs. The selected tab changed from Helping in the kitchen and the task media container changed its description; the visible media was black in that browser state, so the specific action in the clip was not verified. No order or contact form was submitted.

### Accessibility

The everyday tasks use named tabs; the primary order is a link. Several media regions reported Unable to play media in accessibility output. Video captions, keyboard access and reduced-motion treatment were not tested.

## Interpretation

- Let the physical artifact dominate the first impression, then provide task and specification evidence.
- Keep price and fulfillment timing beside the purchase action.
- Treat a product demo as evidence only when its media actually plays.

Useful for physical products where industrial design and price are core buying questions.

Risk: A cinematic page depends heavily on media playback; failed clips weaken proof of capability. Claimed task performance, shipping and specifications are company statements, not independent tests.

## Limitations

- One cloud Chromium session at a 1363 × 936 desktop viewport; the site can change after this date.
- Tablet, mobile, keyboard-only, screen-reader, color contrast and reduced-motion behavior were not tested.
- Exact font families, animation timing, implementation details and conversion effects were not independently established.
- Some task and hardware media did not play in this browser. The clicked task state changed, but its action footage was not visually verified. The $1,688 and fall 2026 shipping claims were observed, not independently verified.
