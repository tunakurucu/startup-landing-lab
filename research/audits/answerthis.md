# AnswerThis

Inspected: 2026-10-19 · desktop-audit · editorial-platform

https://answerthis.io/

A long research-workflow page builds a case from citation anxiety to a five-stage product tour and institutional reassurance.

## Company evidence

YC: verified-yc; batch: F25.

- desktop: 1363 × 936: rendered hero, section scan and selected interaction
- tablet: not inspected
- mobile: not inspected

## Observations

### Hero

Centered navy two-line Scientific AI headline on white, a YC badge, berry-colored Start For Free action, user avatars and a wide video panel. The video showed a buffering state in the inspected opening.

### Typography

Large neutral sans-serif display and smaller gray body, with berry section labels and numbered feature stages. Exact families unverified.

### Visual System

White page, muted navy/berry accents, occasional photography and dense diagrams of papers, citations and workflow. The page alternates centered claims with split visual explanations.

### Page Sequence

Hero/video → logo row and numerical proof claims → research pain points → verification benefit trio → five product stages → enterprise and source verification → privacy/integrations → testimonials → FAQ → final signup/footer.

### Interaction

Opened the Features navigation menu. It revealed descriptive links to Writer, Library, Search Results, Literature Review and other capabilities. The video was buffering at the first capture; no research query or account creation was attempted.

### Accessibility

The page exposed headings and links, but the menu trigger appeared as a generic container in the accessibility tree. This is an observed semantic concern, not a completed keyboard or screen-reader audit.

## Interpretation

- Sequence a complex platform around the researcher’s actual workflow.
- Make citation and verification doubts explicit early.
- Keep institutional security and collaboration information available after the individual workflow story.

Useful for a multi-step research product with separate individual and institutional evaluators.

Risk: Dense claims and a long page can outrun first-screen clarity. Company-reported paper counts, researcher counts and compliance language were observed, not independently verified. The roundup praised the 2025 page, which may differ from today’s design.

## Limitations

- One cloud Chromium session at a 1363 × 936 desktop viewport on 2026-10-19; the homepage can change.
- Tablet, mobile, keyboard-only, screen-reader, color contrast and reduced-motion behavior were not tested.
- Exact font names, animation timing, implementation libraries and conversion effects were not established.
- A third-party design mention is a selection lead, not an independent conversion or product-quality endorsement.
- The roundup called the then-current AnswerThis its favorite F25 landing page; today’s rendering is assessed separately.
