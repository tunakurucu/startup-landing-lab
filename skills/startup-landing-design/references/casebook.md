# Dated reference casebook

Generated from research/sites.json. Each entry carries its inspection date in the canonical record. These are desktop observations, not current universal claims. Mobile, tablet, exact typefaces and reduced-motion behavior were not verified. Entries marked partial-desktop are selected-region field notes, not complete deep audits.

## PostHog · expressive-world

https://www.posthog.com · partial-desktop

A product suite presented as a place to explore, rather than a stack of interchangeable cards.

Observed: A desktop-like shell surrounds a central window. Navigation icons sit on both sides; the introduction and signup panel share a row. Clicked Ship with PostHog. The selected tab and product story changed, with sample inbox reports replacing the earlier assistant story. A pause control was present but not tested.

Transfer: Give a broad suite a navigable mental model. Let expressive brand elements organize information instead of merely decorating it. Identify illustrative data as illustrative.

Avoid/limit: Desktop chrome competes for attention; nested scrolling needs separate mobile and keyboard testing.

## Resend · typographic-technical

https://resend.com · desktop-audit

Editorial type and generous silence open a long, increasingly concrete product tour for developer email infrastructure.

Observed: At 1363 × 936, a left-aligned two-line serif headline moves from bright white to gray on black; Get started and Documentation sit beneath the brief explanation. A faint light ray gives the largely open right side a focal point. A slim navigation bar stays at the top while scrolling. Clicked Python in the integration code chooser. The main language and nested example tabs both selected Python, and the visible snippet changed to Python. The dashboard tabs and testimonial carousel were seen in the full-page capture but were not operated. No email was sent.

Transfer: Make technical adoption concrete with a short, relevant integration example. A quiet hero can precede dense functional explanation. Use typographic contrast to create identity without obscuring product meaning.

Avoid/limit: Some body text appears subdued against black. The long sequence and dense product windows demand careful small-screen pacing. A full-page capture does not establish the behavior of unclicked tabs or carousels.

## Onlook · canvas-first

https://onlook.com/ · partial-desktop

A visual engineering tool explains itself through the language of a working canvas.

Observed: Centered lightweight sans-serif statement; paired design/demo actions lead into a large editor canvas. Clicked the free-version FAQ question. aria-expanded changed to true. The hero showed “Error loading scene” in this browser; the intended scene was not evaluated.

Transfer: Present the workspace as the product’s explanatory medium. Demonstrate continuity between visual editing and real code. Keep essential messaging and actions usable when a rich scene fails.

Avoid/limit: The observed hero scene failure makes this an instructive resilience case, not an unqualified quality endorsement.

## Mercury · immersive-editorial

https://mercury.com/ · partial-desktop

A cinematic opening connects business finance to the world the founder wants to build.

Observed: Centered statement and signup/demo actions sit over a landscape with a desk in the foreground. A financial-service disclaimer is visible at the bottom. Opened Products. A large multicolumn menu exposed banking, cards, payments and intelligence categories. No account application or demo transaction was submitted.

Transfer: Pair an aspirational opening with concrete product explanation. Keep material product qualifications close to the promise. Give a broad offer a navigable category structure.

Avoid/limit: Foreground text competes with the landscape. Navigation overlay obscured some lower-page review; full-page and responsive inspection still needed.

## Linear · workflow-theater

https://linear.app/ · partial-desktop

Product context occupies the space that generic homepages fill with decoration.

Observed: Large left-aligned sans-serif statement followed by a broad issue-management interface with an agent conversation layered alongside it. Opened Product menu and observed grouped workflow destinations. Escape returned visible keyboard focus to the trigger. App-like hero controls were not functionally verified.

Transfer: Use a recognizable work artifact to make the product concrete. Organize page architecture around the lifecycle of work. Limit visual accents so product state carries emphasis.

Avoid/limit: A rich hero can imply more interactivity than exists. Do not copy controls unless they have useful behavior.

## Firecrawl · functional-entrance

https://www.firecrawl.dev · desktop-audit

A technical API turns its main operation into the homepage entrance.

Observed: Centered headline, orange emphasis and an operation field with Search/Scrape/Map/Crawl controls; output preview follows immediately. Clicked Scrape. The action destination changed to a scrape-mode playground URL and the preview displayed structured output. No remote scrape was submitted.

Transfer: Show the shape of input and output before listing features. Let technical affordances become a visual motif. Interrupt long neutral sections with one purposeful color field.

Avoid/limit: Benchmark claims belong to the company and were not independently validated. Pale microtext and extensive scrolling deserve accessibility checks.

## Raycast · workflow-theater

https://raycast.com · desktop-audit

A small recurring command surface makes a large productivity suite feel coherent.

Observed: Centered short headline and download action against black, followed by a launcher demonstration. Full-page composition inspected. A workflow button seen during initial load could not be located after hydration, so its behavior is not verified. Download actions were not activated.

Transfer: Use one interaction grammar across many capabilities. Alternate overview and close-up product views. Make platform requirements visible near the action.

Avoid/limit: Initial-load and hydrated controls differed in this browser. Large blank intervals in a full-page capture may include unrevealed scroll content; do not infer animation implementation.

## Stripe · editorial-platform

http://stripe.com · desktop-audit

Enterprise breadth is organized through business models and implementation paths.

Observed: A broad left-aligned typographic statement overlays a colorful diagonal field; signup actions and a logo strip form a clear lower boundary. Opened Products. The expanded navigation grouped payment capabilities and revealed individual product destinations. Solution demonstrations were not tested.

Transfer: Structure a broad platform by customer decisions. Use a large visual field to create direction without enclosing every sentence in a card. Separate business narrative from developer integration detail.

Avoid/limit: This mature platform’s breadth is inappropriate for a narrow early-stage product. Homepage claims were observed, not independently verified.
