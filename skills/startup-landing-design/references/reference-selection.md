# Select for the decision, not the industry label

Match references to visitor doubts. A developer asks what goes in, what comes out, and whether integration is credible. A consumer asks what experience becomes possible and how much effort it takes. An operations buyer asks what changes in the workflow and how they stay in control.

Use a three-row decision record:

| Role | Required record |
| --- | --- |
| Product explanation | Observed artifact → visitor doubt answered → original artifact to build |
| Brand composition | Observed hierarchy → product fit → original composition |
| Counterexample | Attractive choice → why inappropriate here → constraint to retain |

Examples: transfer Resend's integration-before-feature-depth principle without its serif-on-black treatment. Transfer PostHog's navigable mental model without desktop icons or hedgehogs. Transfer Mercury's connection between ambition and product without its landscape, bank claims or signup form.

Avoid selection by fame, funding or a badge. YC membership verifies cohort metadata, not design quality. The casebook has desktop evidence only, dated 2026-10-18. Mobile and motion gaps are not permission to improvise factual descriptions.
