A family counts once per page when it is the first or second most-used family. "Open licence" means the family is published on google/fonts under the SIL OFL, Apache-2.0 or UFL; a proprietary family is listed so you know what the page used, not so you copy it.

Use this to pick faces with evidence behind them, and to avoid the ones everyone uses unless you have a reason (TY-10).

## What this says

- Open-licence families account for 56% of first-or-second families; system stacks for 8%; the rest are proprietary or self-hosted custom faces.
- Inter alone is on 26% of pages and Geist on 13%: both read as default. A monospace face as the second family is common on developer pages and is a role, not decoration.
- A serif display over a sans body is rare in this sample; where it appears it is a deliberate editorial or consumer choice, which is why TY-10 and CO-03 ask for the reason.
- Families are computed stacks as rendered, not verified licences; confirm the licence of any face before shipping it.
