# Brief: small-run zine printing for independent artists

Staplepress prints small runs of zines and art books, 25 to 500 copies, for independent artists. You upload a print-ready PDF, and within two working days we post a photo of your proof straight off our press, before anything is bound.
Artists and small-press publishers use it, usually on a laptop with their PDF open; there is no separate buyer.
It replaces copy shops that print badly and big online printers that need 1,000 copies and never show a proof.
Uploads start at https://staplepress.example/start. Prices begin at $2.10 a copy for 50 copies of a 24-page A5 zine, black ink on uncoated paper; colour, other sizes and larger runs cost more.
We have printed for about 120 artists since last year but have no permission to name them or show their work. We cannot print full-bleed covers yet.
It should feel like it was made by people who run a press, not by a printing marketplace.
