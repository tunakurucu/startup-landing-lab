What the states tell you

From queued to completed

Queued means work has been accepted into the example. Running marks an attempt. Completed means that attempt succeeded.

When an attempt fails

Retrying allows another attempt under the example policy. Failed is terminal: the allowed attempts have been exhausted. The job ID stays the same.

Example policy only. No throughput measurements, customer claims or delivery guarantees are implied. Production persistence, crash recovery and duplicate handling must be checked against the real package.
