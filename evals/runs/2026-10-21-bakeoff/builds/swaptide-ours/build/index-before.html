Every swap reaches you already checked

For nurse managers and directors of nursing: you see each swap before it happens, with the rule checks on it, and you make the call.

Who decides?
The unit manager, every time. Each swap waits for you to approve or decline it in one tap. Swaptide shows what it found; it never approves a swap on its own.
What gets checked?
Staffing rules: skill mix, such as a charge-trained nurse on the shift, and hours limits. The checks run before the swap reaches you.
Does it override our union agreement?
No. It flags; it doesn’t decide. Your agreement and your decision stand as they are.
Does it handle payroll?
No. Payroll stays exactly where it is today.
Who buys it?
Nurse managers and directors of nursing, from a department budget. Nurses on the unit use it on their phones.
