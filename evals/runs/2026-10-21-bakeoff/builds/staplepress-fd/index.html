How a job goes

Nothing gets folded, stapled or trimmed until you've seen the printed sheet.

  1. Upload a print-ready PDF

    Tell us how many copies you want, from 25 to 500, and whether it's black ink or colour.

  2. We print a proof

    Within two working days we run your pages on our press and post a photo of the sheet.

  3. You check it

    Look at the real ink on the real paper. Approve it, or send a corrected PDF.

  4. We print, staple and send

    The whole run is printed, folded, stapled through the spine and trimmed, then packed for you.

What we can't print

Better you hear it here than after you've uploaded.

Full-bleed covers

We can't print cover art that runs off the edge of the page yet. Leave a white margin around your cover artwork.

Fewer than 25 copies

Below 25, setting up the press costs more than the copies are worth to you. A good copy shop is the better call.

More than 500 copies

Our runs stop at 500. Past that, a large printer built for thousands is the right place for your book.

Why a press, not a shop

Copy shops are quick, but the blacks print grey and the pages drift. Big online printers want 1,000 copies and never show you a proof. We print the runs in between, on our own press, and show you the sheet before we bind a single copy.

We've printed for about 120 artists since last year. You won't find their names or their work on this page. It's theirs, and we haven't asked to show it.
