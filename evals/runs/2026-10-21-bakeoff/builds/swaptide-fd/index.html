Swaptide takes shift swaps off the break-room whiteboard and out of the group text. Nurses post and pick up swaps on their phones. Skill mix and hours limits are checked first, then the unit manager approves or declines before anyone works a shift nobody signed off on.

The same swap without it

It lives in a group text and a scrawl on the whiteboard. The manager finds out on Saturday at 1900, when Marcus walks in instead of Priya, and only then learns whether the shift still has a charge nurse.

Checked before it reaches the manager

You enter your unit's staffing rules once. Every swap is checked against them the moment someone picks it up, so you're never doing the arithmetic at 0700.

  • Skill mix

    The shift keeps the people it needs: enough charge-trained RNs, and any other competencies you require on that shift.

  • Hours limits

    Weekly hours, consecutive shifts and time between shifts, against the limits your unit has set.

Over the hours limitJordan: 48 → 60 h, limit 56

When a swap breaks a rule, it still comes to you, marked with the rule and the numbers. Approving or declining stays your decision.

What doesn't change

  • The manager decides every swap. Swaptide never approves one on its own.
  • Your union agreement and unit policy still govern swaps. Swaptide checks the limits you enter; it doesn't rewrite them.
  • Pay stays in your payroll system. Swaptide doesn't handle pay or timecards.

For nurses

Usually on a break, usually with ten minutes left

  • Post a shift you can't work, or pick up one a colleague posted, from your phone.
  • See where your swap stands: posted, picked up, approved or declined.
  • Get told when the manager decides, instead of checking the whiteboard on your way out.

For the unit manager

Usually between handoff and the first meeting

  • Every swap reaches you before the shift, not after it's been worked.
  • Skill mix and hours are already checked when you open it.
  • Approve or decline in one tap. Both nurses hear the answer from Swaptide, with a record of who agreed to what.
