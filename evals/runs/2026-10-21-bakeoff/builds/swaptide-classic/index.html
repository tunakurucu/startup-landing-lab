What it replaces

The whiteboard, the group text, and the swap you hear about at 06:45.

Most units already swap shifts all the time. The trouble is where it happens: in three places at once, and usually somewhere the manager can't see until the shift has already changed.

Swaptide puts the post, the pickup and the check in one place, and puts the decision where it belongs: with the unit manager, before the shift.

How a swap moves

Four steps. Two nurses, one check, one tap.

Nobody fills in a form at the nurses' station. Each step fits in the gap between call lights, and the manager only gets involved once there's something to decide.

PostPick upCheckDecide
  1. 01Nurse

    Post the shift

    Pick the shift you need covered and post it from your phone, on your break, on days or nights.

  2. 02Colleague

    Someone picks it up

    Nurses on the unit see open swaps in one list and pick one up. No thread to scroll back through.

  3. 03Swaptide

    The rules get checked

    Skill mix and hours limits are checked against your unit's staffing rules. The result travels with the request.

  4. 04Manager

    The manager decides

    Approve or decline in one tap. Both nurses see the answer. Nothing changes until the manager says so.

For nurse managers and directors

You still make the call. You just make it before the shift, not after.

Swaptide doesn't approve anything for you. It gets each swap to you early, with the staffing questions already answered, so the decision takes a tap instead of a phone call.

What stays the same

It sits inside how your unit already works.

Swaptide handles the swap request and the checks. Everything else stays where it is today.

  • Your agreementsUnion and contract terms aren't changed or overridden.
  • Your decisionEvery swap waits for the manager. No auto-approval.
  • Your payrollSwaptide doesn't handle pay or timekeeping.
Built around 07:00 and 19:00

A swap posted at 03:12 shouldn't depend on someone remembering it.

On twelve-hour shifts, the nurse who needs a swap and the manager who approves it are often awake at opposite ends of the day. Swaptide holds the request, with its checks, until the manager is on.

  1. 03:12Dana posts her Thursday night from the break room.
  2. 03:40Marcus, also on nights, picks it up.
  3. 03:40Skill mix and hours are checked against the unit's rules.
  4. 07:00Handoff. Nobody has to mention it in report.
  5. 08:10The manager opens it with the checks attached, and decides.
