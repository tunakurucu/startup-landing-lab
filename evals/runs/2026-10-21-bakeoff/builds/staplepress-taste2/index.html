How a job goes

  1. First, you upload the PDF.

    Single pages in reading order, at the finished size. We check the page count, fonts and margins the day it arrives.

  2. Then we print a proof.

    One full copy’s sheets, on the same paper and ink your run will use.

  3. You see it in two days.

    A photo straight off the press goes up on your job page. Ask for a change, or say yes.

  4. Only then do we bind.

    We print the run, fold, staple, trim and post it. Nothing is bound before you have seen it.

Questions

How do I set up my PDF?

Export single pages in reading order at the finished size: 148 × 210 mm for A5. Embed your fonts and keep text at least 5 mm inside the edge. For black-ink jobs, use plain black rather than a four-colour black.

If your page count is not a multiple of four, we add blank pages at the back and show them on the proof.

Can you print to the edge of the cover?

Not yet. Every page keeps an unprinted border of at least 5 mm, covers included. If your artwork runs to the edge, the proof shows exactly how it prints with the border, before anything is bound.

What if the proof is wrong?

Say so on your job page. If the fault is ours (a streak, a skewed page, the wrong paper), we fix it and post a new photo. If you want to change the file, upload a new PDF and we proof it again, which takes another two working days.

How long does a whole run take?

Two working days to the proof photo. Once you say yes, most runs are printed, folded, stapled and posted within five working days.

Do you print colour or other sizes?

Yes. A5 in black ink is our standard. Colour, A4 and larger runs cost more; the calculator above shows the price as soon as you choose them.

Who have you printed for?

About 120 artists and small-press publishers since last year. We don’t name them or show their work without their permission, so there is no client list here. Ask us and we will tell you what we have printed that is like yours.
