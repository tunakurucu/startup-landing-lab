Every swap waits for your tap

  • Checked before it reaches you. Skill mix and hours limits run first, so a flag shows up beside the swap, not on the floor.
  • One tap to approve or decline. A flag is information. You can still approve a flagged swap, or decline a clean one.
  • Your rules still govern. Your union agreement and your decision stand over every swap. Swaptide doesn’t handle payroll.

What the first call works out

  1. Which unit pilots first, and how its swaps run today.
  2. The staffing rules to check: skill mix, hours limits.
  3. Who approves, and who covers when they’re off.
  4. How long the pilot runs and what it costs.
Does Swaptide ever approve a swap by itself?

No. It runs the staffing checks and shows the result. The manager approves or declines every swap.

Does it override our union agreement?

No. Your agreement and the manager’s decision still govern every swap. Swaptide shows the swap and its checks so the decision is made before the shift, not after it.

Does it handle payroll?

No. Swaptide covers posting, picking up and approving swaps. Payroll stays where it is today.
