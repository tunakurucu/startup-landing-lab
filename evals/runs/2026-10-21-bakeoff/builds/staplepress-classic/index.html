How it works

Four steps. One of them is you looking.

You already have the PDF open. That's most of the work. The rest happens on our press, and you see it before it's finished.

  1. 01You

    Upload a print-ready PDF

    Start at staplepress.example/start. Zines and art books, 25 to 500 copies.

  2. 02Us

    We print your proof on our press

    Not a screen preview and not a mock-up. Your actual pages, printed on our press.

  3. 03Within 2 working days

    We post a photo of the proof

    Straight off the press, before anything is bound. Put it next to the PDF on your laptop and check what the ink actually did: the blacks, the fine lines, the type near the edge.

  4. 04Us

    We print and bind the run

    Then the rest of your copies come off the press, and get bound once you've seen what they look like.

Before you upload

What we can't do yet.

A press is a set of real limits. Here are ours, so you find out now and not on the proof.

  • Covers

    No full-bleed covers yet.

    We can't print a cover where the artwork runs right off the edge of the page. Design your cover with a white border all the way round, or wait until we can.

  • Run size

    25 copies at least, 500 at most.

    Our runs start at 25 copies and stop at 500. If you need fewer or more than that, we're not the right press for the job.

  • Other people's work

    We won't show you anyone else's zine.

    We've printed for about 120 artists since last year. We don't have their permission to name them or show their work, so we don't. The only sheet on this page is our own test page.
