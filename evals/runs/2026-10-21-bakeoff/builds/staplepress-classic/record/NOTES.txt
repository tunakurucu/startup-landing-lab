Staplepress home page: working record
=====================================

Skill: high-end-visual-design (/home/claude/bk/skills/classic/SKILL.md)

Direction ("variance engine" roll)
- Vibe: Editorial Luxury. Warm paper (#F4EFE4), espresso ink (#221C16), one spot colour
  (press red #C2452D), sage status dot, fixed multiply film-grain overlay. It fits "made by
  people who run a press" better than glass/SaaS archetypes.
- Layout: Editorial Split hero (big serif headline left) with a Z-Axis Cascade on the right:
  a proof "photo" on the press bed and an example job ticket, stacked and rotated (-3deg / 2deg).
  Asymmetric offset grids for the steps, the comparison (bento) and the limits.
  Everything collapses to one column below 768px, with no rotation or overlap.
- Type: serif stack Iowan Old Style / Palatino Linotype / Palatino / TeX Gyre Pagella.
  Mono stack SF Mono / Menlo / Consolas / DejaVu Sans Mono for job-ticket labels.
  No banned fonts. Verified via CDP getPlatformFontsForNode: Pagella + DejaVu Sans Mono render here.
- Components: double-bezel tray+sheet cards, pill CTAs with a nested arrow island,
  floating island nav (not edge-to-edge), hamburger that morphs to an X, full-screen blurred
  overlay with staggered link reveal, eyebrow pills, custom cubic-bezier (0.32,0.72,0,1).
- Reveals use IntersectionObserver plus transform/opacity only. Content is fully visible
  without JS, and prefers-reduced-motion is honoured.
- Deviation from the skill: the skill's entry fade uses blur, but its own guardrail says to
  animate only transform/opacity, so I dropped the blur. Mobile section padding is 4.5rem
  rather than py-8, which is too tight for this content.

Content decisions (sticking to the brief)
- No artist names, testimonials or work samples. The only "zine" shown is a drawn house test
  sheet, captioned "Illustration · our own test sheet". A limits card explains why
  (about 120 artists, no permission).
- Full-bleed covers are stated as not possible yet.
- Price: from $2.10 a copy for 50 x 24pp A5, black on uncoated. The run total of $105 is simple
  arithmetic. Colour, other sizes and larger runs are listed as costing more, with no
  invented numbers.
- Every CTA links to https://staplepress.example/start. No invented address, turnaround for
  shipping, paper names or binding specs.

Verification (record/verify.mjs, Playwright Chromium, file:// URL)
- Viewports 1440, 820, 390, 320: no horizontal overflow, no console errors, zero network
  requests, all reveals fire, the menu opens (aria-expanded=true) and closes on Esc,
  axe-core reports 0 violations. The page also renders with JS disabled (shots/nojs-full.png).
- Fixes made during verification: the hero headline was too large and pushed the lede below
  the fold; a floating chip covered the ticket price, so I removed it; the proof caption was
  hidden under the ticket, so I moved it to the top; ink-3 contrast was 4.3:1, so I darkened it;
  removed aria-label on <p>; the eyebrow overflowed at 320px; fixed an awkward
  "1,000-" line break.
- Note: the fixed nav shows mid-page in full-page screenshots. That is a capture artifact
  only.
