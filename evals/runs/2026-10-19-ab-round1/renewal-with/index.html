What we can show you, and what we can’t yet

  • No customer quotes or logos. None are approved for publication, so the sample record is the evidence on this page.
  • No savings figure. We have not measured one we can stand behind, so we don’t print one.
  • Security questions belong in the consultation. Ask where contract data is kept and who can see it; we won’t point to a certification we don’t hold.
  • The clause is still read by lawyers. Ledgerlight keeps the record of the deadline and the decision; interpreting the wording stays with your team.
