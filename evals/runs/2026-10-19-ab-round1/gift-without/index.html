How it works

Three moments, the same way it already happens with friends — just without someone fronting the whole thing and keeping score.

  1. 1

    Someone starts the gift

    Name who it’s for, what you’re getting, and roughly what it costs. That’s the whole setup.

  2. 2

    Friends add what they can

    Share it with the group. Each person puts in an amount that works for them, with a note for the card if they like. There’s no fixed share to live up to.

  3. 3

    The organizer sees what’s left

    One view shows who’s in, how close you are, and what’s still needed — so you know whether to buy, nudge, or trim the plan.

    Questions

    Do I need to enter payment details to try it?

    No. The example on this page runs entirely in your browser. You don’t sign in, nothing is charged, and nothing you type is sent anywhere. Reloading the page clears it.

    Does everyone have to put in the same amount?

    No. Each person adds what they can. The organizer sees the running total and what’s still needed, plus what an even split of the remainder would look like — useful as a guide, not a rule.

    What does the organizer see?

    Who has joined, the total so far, how far you are from the cost of the gift, and the notes people left. You can see all of that in the example above.

    What if we don’t reach the full amount?

    That’s exactly what the “still needed” number is for. The organizer can see the gap early and decide whether to nudge the group, invite a few more people, or pick a slightly smaller gift.

    Is Poolday available now?

    Poolday is a new, small project and still early. The example above shows how a pool works today; the full app is on its way.
