# Brief: background jobs for Node

I maintain an open-source Node queue called Drumbeat for work that shouldn't block a request.
The site should help a developer understand a job's state from enqueue through retry or failure.
Show one safe example that works locally without a server, account or API key.
I want people to read the docs and consider installing the package.
Keep the page credible to experienced engineers, but do not claim unmeasured throughput.
We have no customer logos or testimonials.
I haven't decided whether the primary audience is solo developers or platform teams.
Use the startup-landing-design skill to decide, build and test a page.
