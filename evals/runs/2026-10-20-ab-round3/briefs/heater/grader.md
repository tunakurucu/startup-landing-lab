# Brief: water heaters that wait for cheap power

Tankshift is a controller an electrician adds to an electric water heater so it heats when power is cheapest.
Homeowners care about the bill, but electricians and utility rebate programmes decide whether it gets installed.
Savings depend on each household's tariff and usage, so I won't print a number we can't back up.
Let a visitor try a day's heating schedule against peak and off-peak hours, labelled as an example.
The main action is joining the install list for our first region, but there's no backend and the region isn't public.
We have one utility pilot we can't name, a safety listing still in progress, and no customer stories.
