# Brief: a sunrise lamp pre-order

Morrow is a bedside lamp that wakes you with thirty minutes of rising light: no app, no screen, one dial.
We have a working prototype but no product photos, only CAD renders that will change.
People should reserve one with a refundable $20 deposit, but checkout isn't connected, so nobody can pay today.
The first batch is planned for next autumn, and I don't want to promise a date we might miss.
Visitors can't hold the lamp, so let them get a feel for the wake-up light on the page.
We have no reviews, no press and no sleep-science claims we can stand behind.
