# Brief: cross-stitch patterns from photos

Crossling turns a photo into a cross-stitch pattern you can edit square by square, in real thread colours.
The canvas is the product, so visitors should edit or recolour a small pattern on the page, not look at pictures of one.
Every example must be original artwork; I can't use other people's photos or anyone's characters.
Hobbyists are most of our traffic, but pattern sellers are likelier to pay, and they'll ask about commercial use, which we haven't decided.
The main action is opening the beta editor, but it doesn't have a final address yet.
We have no reviews, pattern counts or community to point to.
Decide on a direction, build and test the page.
