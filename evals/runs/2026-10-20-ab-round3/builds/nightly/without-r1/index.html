Lamplit is an iPhone app that asks you one small question each evening, around the time you'd put a lamp on, and keeps your answers on your phone.

A week of answers

What a week looks like in the app. Six nights are made up. Tonight's is yours.

    How it works

    There isn't much to it, which is the idea.

    One question, in the evening.
    A notification arrives at a time you pick. The question is small and specific: who you talked to, what you ate, where you were at three.
    A sentence is enough.
    There's no word count and no streak to protect. If you skip a night, the next question still comes.
    Answers stay on your phone.
    Your answers are stored on your device. You can read back any week, and delete anything whenever you like.
