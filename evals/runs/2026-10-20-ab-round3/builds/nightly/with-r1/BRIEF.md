# Brief: one question a night

Lamplit is an iPhone app that asks one small question each evening and keeps your answers on your phone.
Most visitors come from short videos on their phones, so design for the small screen first.
We're in TestFlight with about 200 open seats and no App Store listing, so no store badges or ratings.
The main action is asking for a TestFlight invite, and there's nowhere to collect requests yet.
Let a visitor answer tonight's question and see a sample week, with nothing leaving the browser.
Our privacy design hasn't been audited, so promise no more than "stored on your device".
We have no reviews, therapist endorsements or wellbeing claims, and it shouldn't read like therapy.
Use startup-landing-design to choose a direction, build the page and verify it.
