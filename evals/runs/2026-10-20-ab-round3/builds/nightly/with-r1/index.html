Your answers are stored on your device.

That is the promise, and it is the only one we make about privacy right now. Our privacy design has not been audited by anyone outside the team, so we do not claim more than we can show you.

The sample on this page works the same way: it runs in your browser, and nothing you type is sent anywhere.

There is no sign-in on this page and no form that sends data. Reload it and your line is gone.

The questions are small on purpose.

Each one asks about something that happened today: what you ate, who you talked to, what you noticed. A line or two answers it, and a blank evening is allowed. Lamplit is not therapy and does not try to be. It is closer to a note you leave for yourself.

What happens after you ask

  1. Your mail app opens with a one-line request. Send it as it is, or add a word about why.
  2. We reply with a TestFlight link. Invites go out by hand, so it can take a few days.
  3. Install TestFlight, then Lamplit. TestFlight is Apple's free app for beta software. Lamplit needs an iPhone.
