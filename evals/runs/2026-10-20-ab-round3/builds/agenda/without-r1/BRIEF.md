# Brief: council agendas in plain language

Commonread turns city council agendas into short plain-language summaries and alerts residents when their street or topic comes up.
It's a small nonprofit project and must read as neutral, never taking sides on council decisions.
A model drafts each summary and a volunteer checks it, and residents should be able to open the original agenda item.
Residents are who we serve, but city clerks and local funders will also judge the page.
The main action is signing up for one neighbourhood's alerts, and there's no backend for that yet.
About a third of our pilot city speaks Spanish at home, and summaries are English-only so far.
We have no partner cities, funders or usage numbers to show.
Decide on a direction, build and test the page.
