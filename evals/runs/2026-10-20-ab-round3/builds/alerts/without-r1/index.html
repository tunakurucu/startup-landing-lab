Why we lead with the examiner

The hard question is about closed alerts

An alert that becomes a SAR has a narrative. An alert that was closed often has a spreadsheet row and a memory. Casefold asks for the reason at the moment of the decision, from the person who made it, and keeps it with the evidence.

Evidence is recorded as it is checked

Each check is its own entry: what was looked at, what was found, who looked and when. Attachments sit on the entry they support. Rebuilding a file from email and screenshots a year later is not something anyone should have to do.

One record per alert

The alert, the reviewer, the checks, the decision, the second review and the follow-up are a single case. Not a ticket here, a note there, and a PDF somewhere on a shared drive.

Where it sits

Your monitoring system

Raises alerts, as it does today.

Casefold

Opens a case for each alert. Assigns it, records the checks, captures the decision and reason, and closes the file.

Your files

SAR filings, CTRs and customer records stay where they are. Casefold links to them.

Casefold does not raise alerts, score risk or decide anything. The BSA officer decides. Casefold keeps the record.

For the CEO and the board

Anything that touches compliance gets approved above the BSA officer. These are the questions we expect, answered plainly.

Does this replace our monitoring system or our policies?
No. Monitoring keeps raising alerts and your BSA policy keeps setting the rules. Casefold is the place the review of each alert is written down.
Who can change a case file after it is closed?
Anyone with permission can add to the history. No one can edit or delete what is already there. Each addition carries a name and a time.
Can we see what the examiner would see before we commit?
Yes. The sandbox is a working copy loaded with sample cases. Close a few alerts in it, then export the files and show them to whoever will be in the room during the exam.
What does it cost, and what is the contract?
We price per institution, not per user or per alert, so there is no reason to keep people out of the system. Ask for terms when you ask for the sandbox.

What Casefold does not claim

  • It does not make your bank compliant.

    Your program, your policies and your reviewers' judgment do that. Casefold records the work so the judgment can be followed later.

  • It is not approved or endorsed by any regulator.

    No examiner, agency or auditor has reviewed or signed off on Casefold. If anyone tells you otherwise, they are not speaking for us.

  • We do not have a SOC 2 report.

    We are a small company and have not completed one. We will answer your security and vendor-management questions in writing before you decide anything.
