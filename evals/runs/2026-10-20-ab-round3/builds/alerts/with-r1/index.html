Board briefCasefold, as of October 2026
What it is

A case-file tool for alert reviews. One record per alert: reviewer, evidence checked, decision, reason and history.

What it is not

It does not make the bank compliant and does not replace the BSA program, the monitoring system or the BSA officer's judgment. No regulator has reviewed or approved it, and we do not say otherwise.

Who writes in a record

The assigned reviewer and the approving BSA officer. Every entry carries the name and the time of the person who made it, as in the sample above.

Security status

There is no SOC 2 report yet. We say so up front rather than after procurement. Ask for the current description of controls before the board meeting.

What it costs

Pricing is quoted per institution after the sandbox. There is no public price list yet, and the sandbox itself has no charge.

What the sandbox involves

One email. We set it up by hand and reply with a login. It holds sample alerts only; nothing is connected to the bank's systems and no customer data is involved.
