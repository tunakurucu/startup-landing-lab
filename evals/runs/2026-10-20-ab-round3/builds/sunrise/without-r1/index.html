One dial, and that's the whole interface.

Phones in the bedroom are the thing we're trying to get away from, so Morrow doesn't come with one. There's nothing to pair, update, or log in to. It works the day you unbox it and keeps working if we go out of business.

  • Turn it to choose when you want to be awake.

    A ring of marks around the dial shows the hour. Set it once; it remembers.

  • Thirty minutes before, the light begins.

    It starts as a dim ember you could sleep through, and climbs steadily to a warm, full daylight. We tend to surface somewhere in the middle.

  • Press it, and it's an ordinary bedside lamp.

    Reading light at night, off when you're done. Press and hold in the morning if you want it to stop rising.

What the thirty minutes look like.

Real sunrises don't go from black to white; they go through colour. Morrow follows the same path, from a deep ember to something close to morning through a window. These bands are the colours it passes through.

  1. 0 min

    Off. The room is as dark as you left it.

  2. 5 min

    A faint, deep red glow. Barely enough to see the lamp by. Light sleepers may begin to stir.

  3. 15 min

    Orange, then amber. The wall behind the lamp is lit. You can see the room.

  4. 25 min

    Warm, bright, and still rising. This is the point where most of us give up on sleep.

  5. 30 min

    Full brightness, a warm white. It stays there until you press the dial.

Questions we've been asked.

Is the $20 really refundable?

Yes, in full, whenever you ask, until the lamp ships. You don't need to give a reason. Once deposits open we'll also refund automatically if the first batch slips by more than a season, unless you tell us you'd rather wait.

What will the finished lamp look like?

Close to the drawing above in spirit, probably different in detail. A short stem, a shallow shade that throws light at the wall rather than your face, and one dial. You'll see photographs of the real thing before you confirm.

Why no app?

Because the whole point is to leave the phone outside the bedroom. Everything the lamp needs to know, you can tell it with the dial. There's no Wi-Fi in it, so there's nothing to update and nothing to go offline.

Does the light wake you on its own, or is there a sound?

Light only, in the prototype. We're testing a very quiet chime at the thirty-minute mark for heavy sleepers. If it makes the batch, it will be optional and off by default.

When exactly will it ship?

We're planning the first batch for autumn 2027. We'll give a month once the parts are ordered and the factory has committed, and not before. If you hold a place, you'll hear each time the plan changes.

What will the lamp cost?

We haven't fixed the price, because the final parts aren't chosen. You'll see the full price before you're asked to pay anything beyond the deposit, and the deposit comes off it.
