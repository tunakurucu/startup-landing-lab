# Brief: redesign our homepage

Shiftnote is a shared logbook restaurant managers use to hand over between shifts: what ran out, who called in sick, what needs fixing.
Our current homepage is in this folder, and it looks like every other software site, which restaurant people ignore.
Please redesign it, but keep the Shiftnote name, the logo mark and our two brand colours.
The "trusted by" names and testimonials came with the template; none of those restaurants are customers.
Trials start at https://app.shiftnote.example/signup, which works today, so the main buttons should go there and say so.
Managers mostly read on a phone between services; the owner who pays reads later on a laptop.
We have no screenshots we're proud of and no customer numbers yet.
Decide on a direction, build and test the page.
