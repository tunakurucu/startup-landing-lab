# Brief: renting kitchens after hours

Ovenhour lets licensed commercial kitchens rent their empty hours to small food makers who need a certified space.
Kitchens list idle hours and house rules; makers book a shift and see exactly what equipment they get.
There are no listings yet, so the page can't pretend there's anything to browse.
Kitchens can sign up at ovenhour.example/kitchens, but there's nothing yet for makers, the side I hear from most.
The page has to talk to both sides without feeling like two websites stapled together.
Food safety rules differ by county, and we don't know which counties we'll launch in.
We have no reviews, booking numbers or partner kitchens to name.
