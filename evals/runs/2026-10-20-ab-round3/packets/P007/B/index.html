Shiftnote is a shift logbook for restaurant managers. Write what ran out, who called in and what needs fixing on your phone at close; whoever opens reads it first thing, and every item keeps an owner until it’s handled.

    Five kinds of entry cover most of what gets lost between shifts.

    • 86

      What ran out, and what’s nearly out. So the opener fixes the board before the first table asks.

    • Staff

      Call-outs, swaps and no-shows. Who’s covering, and whether they confirmed.

    • Repair

      Anything broken, and who’s been called. Walk-ins, dish machines, heaters, the one toilet.

    • Cash

      Deposits, over/short, the safe. Where the money is when the closer isn’t.

    • Guest

      Complaints, comps and the regular who’s coming back. What was promised, so it gets kept.

    What stays out

    Schedules, the POS, inventory counts and payroll stay where they are. Shiftnote is the book between shifts, not another system to run.

    It’s built for the manager on a phone at 11 pm and the owner on a laptop on Friday.
