Rezoning eight lots on Oakdale Avenue

What's being decided
Whether to change the zoning of eight lots on the north side of Oakdale Avenue, between 3rd and 5th Street, from "single-family homes only" to "neighbourhood mixed use", which allows homes together with small shops or offices. A private developer asked for the change. The Planning Commission has already recommended it.
What happens at this meeting
A public hearing, then a first vote. If it passes, a second vote at a later meeting would make it final.
How to take part
Speak at the hearing (sign up at the chamber door before 6:30 pm) or email the city clerk before the meeting. Public comments received so far are in the packet.
Cost to the city
Staff say none; the applicant paid the fees.

How each summary is made

We're open about the process because you should know how much to trust what you read, and because city staff and funders ask.

The agenda is posted

The city clerk publishes the packet, usually 72 hours before the meeting, as a PDF. We download it the same day. We only work from the city's own posted document.

A language model writes a first draft

Each item gets a draft summary in a fixed shape: what's being decided, what happens at this meeting, how to take part, cost. The draft is never published on its own.

A volunteer checks it against the original

A trained volunteer reads the draft next to the agenda text, fixes anything wrong or missing, and signs it. Every summary shows who checked it and when. If an item hasn't been checked yet, it says so.

What a summary never does

It never says whether a decision is good or bad, and never recommends how to vote or what to tell the council. It describes what's on the agenda, who it affects and how to take part. Volunteers who are involved in an item, for or against, don't check that item.

Si hablas español en casa

Una de cada tres familias en Linden Falls habla español en casa. Queremos que Commonread sirva también en español, y preferimos decirte con claridad qué está listo y qué no.

  • ✓El aviso por correo (el mensaje que te dice que tu calle está en la agenda) puede llegar en español. Marca la casilla al registrarte.
  • ✕Los resúmenes todavía están solo en inglés. No publicamos traducciones automáticas sin que una persona las revise, y por ahora no tenemos suficientes voluntarios bilingües.
  • ✓El documento original de la ciudad siempre está enlazado, y en las audiencias públicas puedes pedir intérprete al secretario municipal con 48 horas de anticipación.

¿Eres bilingüe y tienes una hora cada dos semanas? Escríbenos a hola@commonread.org.

In English

About a third of Linden Falls households speak Spanish at home. Alert emails can be sent in Spanish now. Summaries are English-only until we have enough bilingual volunteers to check translations; we don't publish unchecked machine translation. If you can help, write to hola@commonread.org.

What we ask of a city

  • Nothing you don't already do. We work from the agenda packet you post publicly.
  • A contact in the clerk's office we can send corrections to, and who can send them to us.
  • Permission to say "we summarise the City of X's agenda". We never use the city seal or imply the city wrote the summaries.

If there's an error in a summary we fix it within one working day and leave a note on the summary saying what changed.

What would help next

  • Automated sign-up and sending, so a volunteer isn't adding emails by hand.
  • Bilingual volunteers to check Spanish summaries.
  • A second city with a clerk willing to try it, ideally one that posts its packets as text rather than scans.

Talk to us: hello@commonread.org. We'll send the checking guidelines our volunteers use and a sample of corrected summaries.
