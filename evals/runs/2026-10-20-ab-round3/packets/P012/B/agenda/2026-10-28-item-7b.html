Tuesday, October 28, 2026 — 6:30 p.m.

Council Chambers, City Hall, 1 Civic Plaza, Linden Falls

Members of the public may address the Council on any agenda item at the time the item is considered. 7.B.

ORDINANCE NO. 2026-118 — AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF LINDEN FALLS AMENDING TITLE 14 (ZONING) OF THE MUNICIPAL CODE AND THE OFFICIAL ZONING MAP TO REZONE ASSESSOR PARCELS 031-22-004 THROUGH 031-22-011, INCLUSIVE, LOCATED ON THE NORTH SIDE OF OAKDALE AVENUE BETWEEN THIRD STREET AND FIFTH STREET, FROM R-1 (SINGLE-FAMILY RESIDENTIAL) TO MU-2 (NEIGHBORHOOD MIXED USE), AND MAKING FINDINGS PURSUANT TO SECTION 14.90.040 THEREOF (PLN-2026-0412; APPLICANT: THIRD & OAKDALE PARTNERS, LLC).

Recommended action: Conduct the public hearing; adopt Resolution No. 2026-77 making the findings required by Section 14.90.040; waive full reading and introduce Ordinance No. 2026-118 by title only; and direct staff to return the ordinance for second reading and adoption.

Fiscal impact: None to the General Fund. Applicant has paid processing fees in full per Resolution No. 2025-14.

Attachments: (1) Draft Ordinance; (2) Planning Commission Resolution PC-2026-19; (3) Staff Report; (4) Public Comments Received as of 10/16/2026.

8. Business

8.A.

RESOLUTION NO. 2026-78 — A RESOLUTION ESTABLISHING WEEKEND PARKING RATES OF $2.00 PER HOUR AT THE RIVERVIEW MUNICIPAL PARKING LOT (LOT R-1), EXEMPTING HOLDERS OF VALID RESIDENTIAL PARKING PERMITS, AND DIRECTING NET REVENUES TO THE PARKING FACILITIES MAINTENANCE FUND. Staff estimate annual net revenue of approximately $140,000.

Recommended action: Adopt Resolution No. 2026-78.

Funded from the Facilities Capital Reserve, FY 2026–27 Adopted Budget. Anticipated construction period January through March 2027; the branch will remain open during construction.

Recommended action: Award the contract and authorize execution.
