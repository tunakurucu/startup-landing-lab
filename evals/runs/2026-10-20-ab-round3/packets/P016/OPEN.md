# Open the pages of P016

- Page A: serve it with `python3 -m http.server 8161 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory packets/P016/A/site` and open http://127.0.0.1:8161/ ; source in `A/src/`, build log in `build-A.log`.
- Page B: serve it with `python3 -m http.server 8162 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory packets/P016/B/site` and open http://127.0.0.1:8162/ ; source in `B/src/`, build log in `build-B.log`.

Screenshots taken by the operator at 1440, 768, 390 and 320 wide are in `shots/A/` and `shots/B/` with a mechanical `probe.json` beside them. Judge the pages themselves; the screenshots are a convenience.
