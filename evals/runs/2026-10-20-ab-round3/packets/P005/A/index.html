One patient is offered the slot at a time, so nobody is booked twice.

Chairtime works down your waitlist in order. Each person gets a reply window; a NO or silence passes the slot to the next person. The first YES books the slot and the texting stops.

  • Who gets texted first follows the order of your waitlist and what each person said they are waiting for (days, times, a particular therapist).
  • The reply window is set per clinic. In the example above it is fifteen minutes.
  • Your front desk can see every offer and reply, and can stop the offers at any point.

What a text contains, and what Chairtime keeps

A text carries your clinic's name, the time on offer, the therapist's first name and the reply options. It does not mention a diagnosis, a visit note or a balance. Chairtime keeps a patient's name, mobile number and the days and times they are waiting for.

Got a text from Chairtime? Here is what it is and how to reply.

Your physical therapy clinic uses Chairtime to offer cancelled appointment times to patients who asked to be told about openings. The text comes from Chairtime on the clinic's behalf.

  • 1It names your clinic and offers one date and time. If the clinic named is not yours, ignore it.
  • 2It never asks for a card number, insurance details, a password or anything about your health. A text that does is not from Chairtime.
  • 3Unsure? Call the clinic at the number you already use. The front desk can see the offer on their side.

How to reply

YES
Takes the appointment. You get a confirmation text with the time and address.
NO
Passes it to the next person. You stay on the waitlist for the next opening.
CANCEL
After a YES, gives the slot back so it can be offered to someone else.
STOP
Ends all Chairtime texts for you. Tell the clinic if you want them again.

If you do not reply inside the clinic's reply window, the offer simply moves on. There is nothing to undo.
