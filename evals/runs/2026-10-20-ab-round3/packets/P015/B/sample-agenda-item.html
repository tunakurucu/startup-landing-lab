City Council · Regular Meeting Agenda

Tuesday, 21 October 2026 · 6:00 p.m. · Council Chambers, City Hall

Posted by the Office of the City ClerkAgenda packet, pages 41–63

Item 6.B · Ordinance No. 26-114

An ordinance amending Chapter 17 of the City Code to change the zoning classification of parcels at 1400–1460 Elm Street (Parcel Nos. 0412-118 through 0412-123) from R-2 (Two-Family Residential) to MX-1 (Neighborhood Mixed Use); second reading and final adoption.

Recommendation

Staff recommends approval. The Planning Commission voted 6–1 to recommend approval on 9 September.

Summary of the staff report

The six parcels front Elm Street between 14th Street and 15th Street. The applicant requests MX-1 to permit ground-floor commercial use with residential units above, to a height of three storeys. The R-2 district permits single-family and two-family dwellings only. The request is consistent with the Comprehensive Plan designation of Neighborhood Center for the Elm Street corridor.

Public comment

Public comment will be taken prior to the vote. Speakers sign up with the Clerk from 5:30 p.m. and are limited to three minutes.
