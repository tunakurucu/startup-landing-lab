      Build-your-own nights

      Tacos, baked potatoes, rice bowls. Everyone gets the same dinner, just assembled differently, so there's one pan to wash and no second meal to cook.

      Keep it apart

      One tap moves the sauce, the onions or the green bits to the side of the plate for whoever needs that tonight.

      Repeat the wins

      When a dinner gets eaten without a fight, mark it. Pantrywise brings it back before anyone has time to miss it.

      Less in the bin

      The half bag of spinach gets used on Tuesday instead of thrown away on Sunday. Things that go off first get planned first.

      What it costs

      Pantrywise is free. Later there will be a family plan with shared lists, so whoever is at the shop sees the same list as whoever is at home. We haven't set a price for that yet, and the waitlist will hear first.

      Questions people ask

      Is it on Android?
      iPhone first. Android is planned after that. If you're on Android, join the waitlist anyway and we'll let you know when it's ready.
      Where do the dinners come from?
      We write them ourselves for weeknights: short ingredient lists, thirty minutes or under, and things kids actually eat. No long back-stories above the method.
      Do I have to list everything I own?
      No. Tap the things that go off and the few staples you cook with. Oil, salt, pepper and butter are assumed unless you tell us otherwise.
      What if we don't like a night?
      Swap it. Pantrywise offers another dinner from the same ingredients, and the shopping list changes with it.
