# Brief: unclaimed property compliance for finance teams

Holdfast tracks a company's uncashed checks and dormant customer balances, and prepares the unclaimed-property reports each US state requires.
It's for controllers and accounting managers at companies with 200 to 2,000 employees, who usually discover the obligation after an audit letter.
Buyers are careful: CFOs and auditors read the page, and a wrong deadline is a fine.
The main action is requesting an assessment call at https://holdfast.example/assessment.
State rules and deadlines change; the product shows them by state, but we are not a law firm and can't give legal advice.
We have no customer logos or audit outcomes to show yet.
