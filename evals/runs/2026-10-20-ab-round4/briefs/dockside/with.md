# Brief: truck arrival slots for warehouse docks

Dockline lets mid-size warehouses publish dock appointment slots, so carriers book an arrival time instead of queuing in the yard.
Warehouse operations managers buy it; carriers' dispatchers use the booking side every day and must find it easy.
It replaces phone calls, a shared spreadsheet and trucks waiting for hours outside the gate.
The main action for warehouses is booking a demo at https://dockline.example/demo; dispatchers are sent to https://book.dockline.example.
Two warehouses are piloting it but can't be named, and we have no measured results yet.
The page must feel like it understands a loading dock, not like generic logistics software.
Use startup-landing-design to choose a direction, build the page and verify it.
