Dispatchers book from a link, and the driver gets a door, a time and a reference

No carrier accounts, no app to install. A dispatcher who has never seen Dockline can book from the link in your email signature; the grid above is that page.

  1. Open your warehouse's booking link

    Every warehouse gets its own page on book.dockline.example. It shows your doors, your receiving hours and today's open hours.

  2. Pick a door and an open hour

    The page only offers doors that take the trailer type, and only hours inside the rules you set. Carrier name and load reference are the whole form.

  3. Give the driver the reference

    The confirmation goes to the dispatcher and, if a number was given, to the driver by text. The gate checks the reference, not a spreadsheet.

What it costs, what setup takes, and what it leaves alone

Priced per warehouse, by door count
One price for the warehouse, set by how many dock doors you publish. We quote it on the demo call; there is no public price list yet, and the two pilots are on pilot terms.
Setup is a door list and your hours
Doors, what each takes, receiving hours and slot length: the sheet above. Then you send carriers the link. No WMS integration is needed to start.
It schedules the door, not the warehouse
Your WMS, your TMS and your yard jockey stay as they are. Dockline decides who arrives when; it does not move trailers or receive freight.
