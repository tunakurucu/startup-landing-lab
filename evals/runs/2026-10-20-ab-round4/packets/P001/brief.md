# Brief: dinners from what's already in the fridge

Pantrywise looks at what a family already has in the fridge and cupboard and plans the week's dinners around it, with a shopping list for only what's missing.
It's for parents cooking for picky kids on weeknights, usually on a phone, often in the kitchen.
The app is free; a family plan with shared lists will cost money later, and the price isn't set.
The main action is joining the waitlist for the iPhone app at https://pantrywise.example/waitlist.
We want people to remember us after one visit; the category is full of grey recipe apps.
There are no users, reviews or press yet, and we can't show real recipes from partners.
