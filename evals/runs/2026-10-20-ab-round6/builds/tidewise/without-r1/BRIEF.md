# Brief: tide and swim windows for sea swimmers

Tidewise is an iPhone app for people who swim in the sea all year: it shows today's safe swim windows at their beach from the tide, wind, swell and water temperature, and warns when a window closes early.
Swimmers use it on the way to the beach, usually in the dark before work; there is no business buyer.
It replaces checking three websites and a tide table, and asking the group chat whether it's "too lumpy today".
The app is in public beta; people join through https://tidewise.example/beta. It covers 40 beaches in the UK and Ireland so far.
We have no reviews or download numbers to share. It is not a lifeguard and must never tell anyone it is safe to swim; it shows conditions and the swimmer decides.
It should feel like the sea at 6 am, not like a weather app.
