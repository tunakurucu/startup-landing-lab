What the map is not: Rackwise shows where samples are and who moved them. It does not measure freezer temperature or raise temperature alarms, so keep the monitoring you use now.

What labs ask before requesting access

Is anyone using Rackwise yet?

Four labs are testing it now. They are not named on this page, and there are no results from them to report yet.

Does it watch freezer temperature?

No. Rackwise keeps track of where samples are and who put them there. Temperature monitoring and alarms stay with whatever you use now.

Who uses it, and where?

Researchers and technicians use it at the freezer, often with gloves on: look the tube up, open the door once, record what went in or came out. Lab managers, who buy it, use the whole map.

What happens when I request early access?

The button opens the early-access request page at rackwise.example. Ask there about setup, how many freezers you can map, and cost.
