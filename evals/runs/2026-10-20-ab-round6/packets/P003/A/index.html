How a review runs

Five steps, the same every time. Your staff work the queue; your compliance officer makes the call.

  1. Find who is due

    Refresh applies the review cycles in your policy to each member's risk tier and puts the members who are due in a daily queue, with the reason each one was selected.

    Done by Refresh

  2. Gather what is on file

    Identification, address and the profile details you already hold are pulled into the review, each with its date, so staff can see at a glance what is current and what is stale.

    Done by Refresh, checked by member-service staff

  3. Ask for what is missing

    The member gets a short request listing only the items you need. They reply online, and the answers arrive in the review for staff to check.

    Member replies, staff review

  4. Decide

    Your compliance officer reads the completed file and makes the risk decision, with their rationale in their own words.

    Your compliance officer, not Refresh

  5. Record and schedule the next one

    The review closes with every step dated and attributed to a person. The next review date follows from the decision.

    Done by Refresh

The risk decision stays with your compliance officer

Refresh organizes the review and keeps the record. It does not score members, change risk tiers or close a review on its own. When an examiner asks who decided, the answer is a named person at your credit union.

Questions your examiner will ask

You will be asked about any system that touches your BSA program. Here is where each answer lives in Refresh.

How do you decide which members are due?
Your written policy sets review cycles by risk tier. For any member, the review file shows the tier, the cycle that applied and the date they were selected.
Who made the risk decision?
A named compliance officer, recorded with the date and their rationale. Refresh does not make or change the decision.
What was already on file, and how current was it?
Each item gathered for the review is listed with its date, so you can show what was relied on and what was refreshed.
What did you ask the member for, and what came back?
Every request and every reply sits in the same file, dated, along with the staff member who checked it.
Are reviews being finished on time?
Reviews that are due, in progress or overdue are visible in one list, so you know where you stand before the exam starts.
Can you show me one from start to finish?
Open the member's review file. It reads in order, from selection to decision, the way the sample at the top of this page does.
