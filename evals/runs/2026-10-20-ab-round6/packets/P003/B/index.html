Examiner: “How do you decide who is due?”

Due dates come from your review schedule, member by member

Your compliance officer sets how often each risk tier is reviewed. Refresh keeps every member's last review date and builds the list of who is due.

Member-service staff work that list each day: who needs a request, who replied, which files are ready for the officer.

Examiner: “What did you ask the member for?”

Members get one short request, for the missing items only

Refresh has already checked what's current, so the request names only what's missing. The date it went out goes on the trail.

Examiner: “Who decided this risk rating?”

Your officer makes the risk call. Refresh writes it down.

Refresh gathers, asks and keeps the record. It never rates a member: a complete file waits for your officer, and the decision goes on the trail with a name, a time and a reason.

Examiner: “Show me what happened, in order.”

Hand the examiner the trail, not a rebuilt spreadsheet

Every review leaves the same record, in the order it happened: why the member was due, what was on file and where it came from, what was asked and when, what came back, and who decided.
