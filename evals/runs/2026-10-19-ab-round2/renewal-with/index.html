Every date points back to the words it came from.

A renewal record is only as good as its reading of the notice clause. In the sample, each field carries the phrase it was read from, so a reviewer can check the deadline against the contract instead of trusting a calendar entry.

Sample clause, written for this page

14.2 Renewal. This Agreement will renew automatically for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term. Notice must be delivered by courier to the address in Schedule 1 and copied by email.

  1. 1Renewal termRenews for 12 months on 31 Mar 2027 if nothing is done
  2. 2Next decisionRenew, renegotiate, or give notice of non-renewal
  3. 3Notice deadline31 Dec 2026: 90 calendar days before 31 Mar 2027
  4. 4Review trailNotice is logged only with the courier receipt and the email attached

The same record, whichever side of the contract you work on.

In-house teams track their own company’s contracts. Outside counsel track deadlines for clients. The fields stay the same; who fills them changes.
