Practise the bars that need it, at a speed that feels easy.

Halfstep is for adults coming back to the piano. Play a piece through once while your phone records. Halfstep circles the bars worth practising and pencils a slower starting tempo beside each one.

A teacher’s pencil, not an examiner’s red pen.

Coming back after years away, you already know how the piece should sound. Your hands are catching up. Halfstep doesn’t give you a mark or a percentage; it tells you where the next ten minutes will do the most good, in the words a patient teacher would write in the margin.

Bars that went fine stay clean. Nothing is marked wrong.
