How a pool runs, Monday to Saturday

The same sample pool as above, at four points in its week.

  1. 1 Monday

    Tom starts it

    $60 of $240

    He names the gift and the total, puts in his own $60, and drops the link in the group chat.

  2. 2 Tuesday–Wednesday

    Friends add what they can

    $155 of $240

    Priya, Sam and Jo each add a different amount and a line for Ines. Nobody is asked to match anyone.

  3. 3 Thursday

    Tom sees what’s left

    $85 still needed

    Three people haven’t opened it. He sends one nudge to those three instead of the whole chat.

  4. 4 Saturday

    The ribbon ties

    $240 of $240

    The last $85 comes in, Tom books the class, and Ines opens one present with seven names on it.

Friends see the notes. Only the organizer sees the numbers.

Chipping in for a friend shouldn’t feel like settling a bill. Everyone in a pool sees who’s in, what they wrote and how close the gift is. Individual amounts stay with the organizer, who needs them to know what’s left.
