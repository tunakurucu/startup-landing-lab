    How it works

    Three steps, about as long as the piece

    1. Record a run-through

      Set your phone on the music stand and play the piece, or the part you’re working on, the way you’d play it for a friend. Stops and restarts are fine.

    2. See your practice map

      Every bar gets a calm “steady” or a gentle “worth a slower pass.” Most of your piece will be the first kind. That’s the point.

    3. Practise slow, then climb

      Each spot comes with a tempo to start at and the steps back up to your own. Loop it until it feels easy, then take the next step.

    Made for coming back

    A practice partner, not an examiner

    You already know how to practise. What’s hard after a break is knowing where to spend twenty minutes. Halfstep answers that one question and gets out of the way.

    • No marks, no grades

      You get a map of your piece, not a score out of 100. Steady bars are the default, not a reward.

    • Slow is a step, not a setback

      A slower tempo is where a spot starts, with a clear way back up to the speed you want.

    • Your piece, your phone

      Record the piece you’re actually working on, on the phone you already have, at whatever piano you play.
