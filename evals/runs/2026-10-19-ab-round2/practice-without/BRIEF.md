# Brief: piano practice

I'm building Halfstep, an app for adults coming back to the piano.
You record yourself playing a piece on your phone, and it shows which bars to practise and how slowly.
The page should feel encouraging, not like a music exam.
Show an example a visitor can try without uploading audio or signing up.
The main action is joining the beta waitlist, but there's no backend for it yet.
We have no user results, teacher endorsements or accuracy numbers.
I haven't decided whether to aim at returning adult learners or their teachers.
Choose a direction, build the page and verify it.
