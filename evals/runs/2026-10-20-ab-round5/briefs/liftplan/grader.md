# Brief: crane lift plans for construction sites

Liftplan turns a crane lift (load weight, crane model, radius, ground conditions) into a checked lift plan that the site's appointed person can sign on a tablet.
General contractors' site managers buy it; appointed persons and crane supervisors use it before every critical lift.
It replaces a spreadsheet template copied from job to job and load charts read off PDFs by hand.
The main action is requesting a pilot on one site at https://liftplan.example/pilot.
It is in pilots with two contractors who cannot be named. It does not replace the appointed person's judgment, and the page must not suggest it does.
The page should feel like it belongs on a construction site, not in a SaaS template.
