# Brief: a checklist for people settling a parent's estate

Settle walks an executor through closing a parent's affairs after a death: which accounts, bills and agencies to contact, in what order, with the letters and documents each one needs.
Adult children who have just become executors use it, usually on a phone, often in the first weeks after a death; there is no professional buyer.
It replaces a lawyer's generic PDF, a notebook of phone numbers and hours on hold.
It is not available yet. We want to collect interest, but there is nowhere to send sign-ups yet.
It covers England and Wales only at launch. It is not legal advice, and the page must say so without hiding it.
The page must be calm and kind without being gloomy, and must never feel like it is selling to someone who is grieving.
