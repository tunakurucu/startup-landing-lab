# Brief: kiln booking and firing log for pottery studios

Kilnlog is a phone app for community pottery studios: members see when the kiln is loading, firing or cooling, claim shelf space in the next firing, and get a message when their pieces come out.
Studio owners pay for it; members use it every week, often with clay on their hands.
It replaces a paper sign-up sheet taped to the kiln room door and a group chat where people ask "is it out yet?" all day.
Studio owners start a free 30-day trial at https://kilnlog.example/start. Members do not sign up on this page; their studio invites them.
Three studios have used it since spring; we have no figures we can publish.
It should feel warm and made by people who throw pots, but a studio owner must still trust it with their schedule.
