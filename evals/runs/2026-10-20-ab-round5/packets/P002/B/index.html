Lift plans that read the load chart for you

Liftplan is a lift plan app for construction sites. Enter the load, the crane, the radius and the ground; it checks them against the load chart and writes the plan your appointed person signs on a tablet.

    Four boxes in. Every number comes off the chart.

    The old plan was a spreadsheet copied from the last job, with capacities read off a PDF chart and typed in by hand. Liftplan takes the same four facts and quotes the chart row itself.

    1. 1

      Load

      Net weight plus everything under the hook: block, slings, shackles, beams. The gross figure is what gets checked.

      net + rigging = gross

    2. 2

      Crane and configuration

      Model, boom length, counterweight, outrigger spread and slew. The chart column is quoted for that exact set-up; change one and the column changes with it.

      one configuration → one chart column

    3. 3

      Radius

      Measured from the slew centre. Rounded up to the next chart row, never interpolated, and the rated capacity at that row is shown beside the gross load.

      radius 11 m → row 12 m

    4. 4

      Ground

      Allowable bearing pressure for the standing, against the heaviest outrigger load and the mat area. A pass on the chart with a fail on the ground is still not issued.

      outrigger load ÷ mat area ≤ allowable

    The appointed person signs. Liftplan never does.

    A lift that passes the chart is not yet a plan. The appointed person classifies the lift, sets the method, and decides whether it happens today. Those boxes stay theirs, on the tablet, and the plan is not issued until they sign.

    Filled by Liftplan

    • Gross load, from net load and rigging
    • Chart row and rated capacity for the configuration
    • Gross as a share of rated capacity
    • Outrigger load, pad pressure and the ground check
    • Flags above the site's thresholds

    Decided by the appointed person

    • Lift categoryBasic, standard or complex
    • Method and sequenceRigging, tag lines, landing, exclusion zone
    • Go or no-go on the dayWind, ground after rain, visibility
    • SignatureOn the tablet, before the lift
