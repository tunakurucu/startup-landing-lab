A member's week with Kilnlog

Members don't sign up or pay. Their studio sends an invite, and from then on the kiln fits into their week like this.

  1. Claim a spot in the next firing

    Open the next firing, see which shelves have room, and tap a spot. Everyone else sees it's taken straight away, so two people can't plan on the same space.

  2. Check the kiln without asking

    Loading, firing or cooling, with the temperature and when it's expected out. The answer is on their phone, not in someone's head.

  3. Get a message when their pieces are out

    When the kiln is unloaded, everyone with work in that firing hears about it, with which shelf and where to pick up.

You run the kiln. Kilnlog keeps the record.

Members can claim space and see the status. The schedule and the firings are yours to set, and every firing is written down.

  • Put firings on the schedule

    Bisque or glaze, the cone, when it loads and when it should come out.

  • Decide how shelf space is shared

    Set how much each member can claim and when claims close before loading.

  • Update the status from the kiln room

    Change it from loading to firing to cooling on your phone, and members see it right away.

  • Keep a log of every firing

    Who had work in it, the cone, when it came out, and any notes you want to remember next time.
