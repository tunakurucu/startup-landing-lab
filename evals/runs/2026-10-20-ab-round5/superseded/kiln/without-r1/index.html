A firing, the way your members see it

Every kiln is always in one of four states. Kilnlog shows the current one first, in a colour you can read from across the wedging table.

Loading

The next firing is open. Members claim a shelf, or part of one, from their phone. First come, first served, same as the sheet, except everyone can see how much space is left.

Firing

Whoever closes the kiln taps once. Members see the cone, the program and the earliest time it could be out. Nobody needs to ask.

Cooling

The slow part. The board says it's still too hot to open, so nobody cracks the lid because they got tired of waiting.

Out

Whoever unloads taps once more. Everyone with a piece in that firing gets one message, with a note on where their work was put.

Made for hands covered in slip

Members use Kilnlog every week, usually mid-throw, with a phone propped against a bucket. So the app asks very little of them.

Publish the schedule, weeks ahead

Set which kiln fires when, at what cone, and how many shelves are open. Members only see firings you've published, and only claim what you've allowed.

No double-booked shelves

A shelf can be claimed once. When the firing is full, the button says so. You can hold shelves back for classes or your own work.

A log you can hand to the next person

Every firing records who loaded, who unloaded, the program, the cone and any notes. Download it as a spreadsheet whenever you want it.
