How a job moves through the press

Nothing gets bound until you've seen your zine printed on our press.

  1. When you're ready

    Upload a print-ready PDF

    Tell us how many copies you want, from 25 to 500, and the size and ink.

  2. Within two working days

    We print a proof and post a photo of it

    The photo is of a real sheet, straight off our press, so you see how your ink actually sits on the paper.

  3. After you've seen it

    We bind the run

    Nothing is bound before you've seen the proof.
