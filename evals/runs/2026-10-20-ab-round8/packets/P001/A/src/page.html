On your proof, page 24 sits next to page 1

The photo shows flat sheets the way the press prints them, not the way you’ll read them. A stapled zine is folded sheets nested inside each other, four pages to a sheet. So a 24-page zine is six sheets, and the outside of the first one carries pages 24 and 1.

To check a proof, add the two page numbers on any side of a sheet. They should make one more than your page count: 25, for 24 pages.

Page counts run in fours. If yours doesn’t, the sheet at the top of this page shows where blank pages would fall.

A proof on runs of 25, not a 1,000-copy minimum
