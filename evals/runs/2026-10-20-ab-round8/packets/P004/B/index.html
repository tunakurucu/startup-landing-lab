Trustees see two names against every Sunday’s total

The finance committee or trustees decide whether to adopt Tallyhall, and they answer for the money afterwards. The ledger shows them each count, who signed it, and what was posted.

  • No count posts until both counters have signed.
  • If a figure changes after someone signs, the signature is cleared and they sign again.
  • The Sunday you just counted above lands here once both have signed.

The questions a careful treasurer, or a committee, asks first.

Does Tallyhall take or move any money?

No. Tallyhall is not a payment processor and does not take online donations. It records the cash and cheques your volunteers count, keeps the ledger and produces the giving statements.

Does it work for us in the UK or the US?

Yes. Tallyhall works for churches and small charities in both, which is why the sample count above switches between pounds and dollars.

Who uses it already?

About 40 congregations. None of them is named here, and we don’t publish figures about them.

How do we try it before the committee decides?

Start the free trial: it runs for 60 days, so you can count with it on the Sundays before the meeting and take the ledger with you.
