    Nobody checks the rules until it’s too late

    Who’s charge on Thursday? Is that Dana’s fourth night in a row? Someone works it out in the hallway, or doesn’t.

    The manager hears at huddle, after the fact

    By the time it reaches the person who signs off, the swap has already happened.

Three places on the unit, none of them a desk

A swap happens in the gaps of a 12-hour shift: the break room at 03:10, the nurses’ station at handoff, a manager between meetings. Swaptide is built for those minutes, on the phone already in a scrub pocket.

You set the limits. Every swap arrives checked against them.

For nurse managers and directors of nursing. Enter the skill-mix and hours limits from your unit policy and union agreement; Swaptide checks each swap against them before it reaches you. Change a limit and watch the sample queue.

What your director, your union rep and your IT team will ask

Does it handle payroll?
No. Swaptide covers posting, picking up and approving swaps. Pay stays where it is today.
Does it override our union agreement?
No. You enter the limits your agreement and unit policy set, and Swaptide shows when a swap breaks one. It doesn’t replace the agreement, change what it says, or decide for you.
What has the pilot shown so far?
One medical-surgical unit has used Swaptide for three months. We don’t have measured results to share yet, so this page doesn’t quote any.
