# Brief: bookkeeping for churches and small charities

Tallyhall helps churches and small charities keep their books: two volunteers count the Sunday offering on a phone and both sign it off, the count posts to the ledger, and at year end it produces each donor's giving statement.
Volunteer treasurers use it every week; the finance committee or trustees decide whether to adopt it.
It replaces a counting sheet, a spreadsheet only the treasurer understands, and a January spent writing giving statements by hand.
The main action is starting a free 60-day trial at https://tallyhall.example/trial. It works for organisations in the UK and the US.
About 40 congregations use it; none can be named and we have no figures to publish. It is not a payment processor and does not take online donations.
The page must earn the trust of a careful volunteer who handles other people's money, without feeling like corporate finance software.
Decide on a direction, build and test the page.
