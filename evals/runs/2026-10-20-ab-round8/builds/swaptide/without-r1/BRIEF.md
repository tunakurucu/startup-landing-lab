# Brief: shift swaps for hospital nurses

Swaptide lets hospital nurses post and pick up shift swaps from their phones, and lets the unit manager approve or decline each swap in one tap, with the staffing rules (skill mix, hours limits) checked before the manager sees it.
Nurse managers and directors of nursing buy it from a department budget; nurses on the unit use it daily, often on a break.
It replaces the whiteboard by the break room, group texts, and swaps that reach the manager after they have already happened.
The main action is booking a pilot call at https://swaptide.example/pilot.
One medical-surgical unit has piloted it for three months; we have no measured results we can share. It does not handle payroll, and the page must not suggest it overrides union agreements or the manager's decision.
It should feel like it understands a 12-hour shift, not like HR software.
