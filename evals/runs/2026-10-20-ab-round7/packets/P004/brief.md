# Brief: knowing where every sample is in a lab's freezers

Rackwise keeps a live map of a lab's −80 °C freezers: every rack, box and tube position, who put it there and when, so a sample can be found in seconds with the door open as briefly as possible.
Lab managers at small biotech companies and university labs buy it; researchers and technicians use it at the freezer, often wearing gloves.
It replaces a shared spreadsheet that is always out of date and handwritten box labels that fade in the cold.
The main action is requesting early access at https://rackwise.example/early-access.
Four labs are testing it; none can be named and we have no figures. It does not monitor freezer temperature, and the page must not imply it does.
The page should feel like it was made by people who have stood at a freezer looking for a box.
