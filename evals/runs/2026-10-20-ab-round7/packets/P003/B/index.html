KYC reviews your examiner can follow

Refresh is periodic KYC review software for credit unions with 20,000 to 200,000 members. It finds who is due, gathers what is on file, asks members only for what is missing and records each step.

Your policy decides who is due. Your officer decides the risk.

You write the review rules once. Refresh applies them to every member and shows which rule put each one on the list.

5Decided

When an examiner asks about a member, the answer is already in the trail

No binder to build the week before. Every review keeps the same five sections, in order.

  • WhoRefresh, a named staff member, the member, or the officer
  • WhenDate and time of each step
  • WhyThe policy rule that made the member due, and the item that was missing
  • DecisionThe officer’s rating and note, in their name
