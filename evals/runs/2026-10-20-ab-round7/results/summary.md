**Target (pre-registered): a mean skill-page total of at least 91. Not met: 83.75** (range 81–86). Round 6 on the same briefs: 84.25. The no-skill pages: 72.6 (round 6 graders: 71.1). The skill page won all four briefs and all 12 grader runs.

**v0.8 did not raise the score on these briefs.** Every per-brief change is within grader noise (below). Per-criterion means for skill pages barely moved: craft 7.67 (7.83), distinctiveness 7.67 (7.83), mobile 8.08 (8.0), first_screen 8.67 (8.5).

**Grader noise, measured for free.** The four no-skill pages were byte-identical in both rounds and graded by different graders: the 12 per-persona differences have a mean absolute size of 2.5 points and a standard deviation of 3.1 (largest: 8, the tidewise designer). One grader's total moves by about ±3 on an unchanged page; a 12-run mean moves by about ±1. Changes smaller than about 2 points in a round mean are not distinguishable from noise with this design.
