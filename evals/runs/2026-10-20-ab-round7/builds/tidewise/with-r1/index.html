Tidewise is an iPhone app for people who swim in the sea all year. It turns the tide, wind, swell and water temperature at your beach into today’s swim windows, and warns you when one closes early.

It shows conditions. You make the call.

Tidewise is not a lifeguard, and it never tells you it’s safe to swim. A swim window is a reading of the tide, wind, swell and water temperature at your beach. Whether you go in is still your decision, made at the water’s edge.

What Tidewise reads for your beach

  • Tide height and times
  • Wind speed and direction
  • Swell height
  • Water temperature

What only you can read

  • Flags and lifeguard advice, where your beach has them
  • Currents, rips and how the water looks when you get there
  • How much cold you can take today, and who is swimming with you

In public beta on iPhone at 40 beaches

Tidewise covers 40 beaches in the UK and Ireland so far. If you swim at one of them, join the beta and read the morning’s windows before you leave the house.
