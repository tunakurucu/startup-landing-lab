# Brief: periodic KYC reviews for credit unions

Refresh helps small credit unions run their periodic know-your-customer reviews: it decides which members are due, gathers what is already on file, asks members for only what is missing, and leaves an audit trail an examiner can follow.
BSA and compliance officers at credit unions with 20,000 to 200,000 members buy it; member-service staff use it daily, and members see only a short request for documents.
It replaces a spreadsheet of review dates, mailed letters and phone calls, and a scramble before every exam.
The main action is booking a demo at https://refresh.example/demo.
Two credit unions are piloting it and cannot be named. It does not make the risk decision; a compliance officer does. We have no measured results yet.
The page must reassure a compliance officer who will be asked about it by examiners.
